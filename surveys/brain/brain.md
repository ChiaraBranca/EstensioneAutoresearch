# Living Survey: brain

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **brain**.

## Brain-Computer Interfaces and Neural Signal Processing
Recent advances in EEG and MEG signal processing have significantly improved the reliability and accessibility of Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs). Machine learning approaches, particularly Random Forest algorithms, have demonstrated robust performance in classifying EEG signals from real and imagery motor activities, even when using consumer-grade hardware[^2609.11695]. To address reproducibility in analyzing neuronal avalanches, the open-source package pyAvalanches provides a standardized pipeline for characterizing spatiotemporal propagation patterns in EEG recordings[^2609.11530]. In P300 speller systems, component anti-synergy has been identified, revealing that adding individually promising components like language models or spatial filters can sometimes degrade performance depending on the underlying EEG pipeline quality[^2609.10961]. Federated learning frameworks like NEXUS-MI further enhance MI-BCIs by coordinating personalization across gateways while reducing communication overhead[^2609.09786]. For clinical applications, EEGBind leverages EEG-centric multimodal binding to accurately classify source-level interictal epileptiform discharges, improving presurgical evaluation[^2609.09728]. Beyond clinical settings, brain-based digital twins are being developed to fuse consumer-grade EEG with kinematic data for real-time motor imagery feedback in sports training[^10.17870_bathspa.00017805]. Additionally, research into infant development links EEG microstates to temperament attributes, offering new insights into early self-regulation and emotional reactivity[^10.7273_000007929].

## Neuroimaging, Registration, and Spatial Analysis
High-resolution neuroimaging and precise spatial registration remain critical for understanding brain structure and function. Cross-modal diffusion transformers, such as CoMA-DiT, are being utilized to augment multimodal brain state decoding by treating paired modalities as mutual generative supervision sources, yielding significant accuracy gains in auditory attention and emotion recognition tasks[^2609.11341]. For MEG data, a novel regression-based framework utilizing Wendland radial basis functions on the cortical surface provides spatially resolved effect estimates, overcoming the limitations of traditional cluster-based permutation tests[^2609.11278]. In model systems, deep learning pipelines now enable rapid, high-resolution deformable registration of Drosophila larval brains, outperforming classical methods in both speed and accuracy[^2609.11240]. Human brain MRI registration has also advanced with two-parameter flow map learning, which directly learns continuous-time non-autonomous ODE solutions to preserve diffeomorphic structure while improving alignment accuracy[^2609.10789]. Furthermore, LUCID integrates multi-view diffusion priors with projection-domain data consistency to resolve missing-cone artifacts in laminographic X-ray nanoimaging of brain tissue, enhancing spatial fidelity for connectomics[^2609.10456]. At the sensory level, spatial transcriptional codes have been shown to govern olfactory receptor choice, precisely aligning sensory maps between the nose and the brain[^10.5281_zenodo.18941307].

## Brain Aging, Neurodegeneration, and Biomarkers
Computational phenotyping of brain aging is accelerating through longitudinal modeling and foundation models. Brain-PACE extends Siamese longitudinal frameworks to directly estimate structural brain aging pace from paired MRI, revealing stronger associations with tau burden and cognitive impairment than indirect cross-sectional methods[^2609.11378]. The BrainTaskonomy framework organizes fMRI foundation model pretraining and adaptation using measured learning relations and priority-guided curricula, significantly reducing prediction errors across diverse tasks[^2609.10518]. Theoretical advances in covariance neural networks (VNNs) provide refined stability bounds and transferability insights for characterizing brain age gaps in neurodegenerative conditions[^2609.10490]. Explainable AI (XAI) is also being integrated into brain-age prediction via automated explanation-knowledge loops that align model explanations with verified neurobiological literature[^2609.09388]. On a structural level, category fluency performance is influenced by gray matter volumes in the DLPFC and IFG, with cultural and age-related variations highlighting the interplay between neural efficiency and cognitive strategies[^10.48617_etd.1671]. At the molecular level, circular RNAs (circRNAs) accumulate in the Drosophila brain in correlation with biological age, serving as stable biomarkers for aging and insulin-related pathways[^10.48617_etd.1568]. Comprehensive arteriovenous metabolic profiling across human organs further quantifies brain-specific metabolite exchange, offering a quantitative atlas for identifying pathological features[^10.5281_zenodo.20652506].

## Brain Tumors, Metastases, and Therapeutics
The diagnosis and treatment of brain tumors and metastases are being transformed by deep learning and targeted delivery systems. For brain metastases, nnU-Net ensembles combined with rule-based post-processing achieve high lesion-wise Dice similarity coefficients, though rigorous out-of-fold audits reveal that some leaderboard gains do not generalize[^2609.11477]. Scale-aware 3D deep learning frameworks that fuse probability maps from different spatial fields of view improve lesion-level precision and reduce false positives in multimodal MRI detection[^2609.10825]. Single-cell spatially resolved atlases using 10x Visium are mapping pan-cancer metastatic brain tumors, providing unprecedented cellular and spatial resolution of the tumor microenvironment[^10.5281_zenodo.21816284]. In Alzheimer's research, the amyloid precursor protein (APP) is increasingly recognized as a universal survival molecule, raising critical concerns about broad-spectrum APP suppression therapies that may disarm the brain's natural repair mechanisms[^10.5281_zenodo.20049820]. Therapeutic delivery across the blood-brain barrier is being advanced through lipid nanoparticle (LNP) formulations encapsulating siRNA targeting TERT in glioblastoma, demonstrating robust gene silencing and cytotoxic effects[^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059253]. Additional LNP platforms loaded with epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG) are being optimized to bypass the BBB and target glioma stem cells[^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059033]. Furthermore, secretomes and extracellular vesicles (EVs) derived from neural crest stem cells inhibit glioblastoma proliferation and normalize the anti-tumor immune environment by modulating NF-κB signaling[^10.48683_1926.00128572]. Finally, RET signaling pathways are being investigated as key mediators in estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer brain metastasis[^10.5281_zenodo.19501083].

## Network Neuroscience and Dynamics
Understanding large-scale brain networks and neural dynamics continues to bridge computational modeling with biological observation. The "Platonic brain bridge" hypothesis proposes that human brain networks can serve as architectural priors for omni-modal models, with cortical networks acting as experts that significantly boost model accuracy[^2609.10947]. Cortical information transfer analysis using Permutation Transfer Entropy on EEG data demonstrates that hemispherical network dynamics during motor execution are conserved across right- and left-handed individuals, refuting assumptions of mirrored lateralization[^2609.10870]. Scalable neuronal reconstruction from petabyte-scale microscopy data is achieved through SkNeXt, a topology-first framework that uses compact SWC skeletons to reduce computational overhead and preserve long-range connectivity in mouse brain datasets[^2609.09832]. Theoretical models of real neurons indicate that criticality emerges naturally as system size increases, resolving tensions between fine-tuning requirements and observed neural activity patterns[^2609.09438]. At the brainstem level, cholecystokinin (CCK) signaling in the nucleus of the solitary tract (NTS) is blunted by high-fat diets, linking central vagal afferent pathways to satiety regulation and obesity-related metabolic disruption[^10.7273_000008411].
