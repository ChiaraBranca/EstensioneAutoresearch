# Living Survey: brain

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **brain**.

## Neural Decoding and Brain-Computer Interfaces

Recent advances in brain-to-language decoding have expanded the field from constrained recognition to text generation and streaming personalized speech, synthesizing developments across invasive and non-invasive measurements while highlighting the need for shared benchmarks and longitudinal studies [^2609.27650]. To address session variability in invasive brain-machine interfaces, Task-Conditioned Latent Alignment (TCLA) stabilizes neural decoding by learning a shared latent space aligned separately for each task condition, significantly reducing failure rates across cross-subject and long-term evaluations [^2609.27441]. In the realm of signal preprocessing, attention-guided conditional adversarial learning (P2P Trans) effectively suppresses electromyographic artifacts in single-channel EEG, demonstrating superior spectral restoration under severe contamination [^2609.26772]. Furthermore, modeling long-horizon EEG sequences benefits from neuroscience-inspired tokenization; Brain-Token Learning replaces fixed temporal windows with microstate-derived brain tokens, capturing quasi-stable large-scale brain states and improving representation learning across affective and clinical datasets [^2609.24324]. Optimizing cross-subject EEG decoding remains challenging due to gradient nonstationarity, but adaptive forgetting controllers like AFOR dynamically adjust second-moment decay coefficients, yielding robust performance improvements over fixed optimizers [^2609.24233]. Developmental neuroscience also explores these techniques, with proposed frameworks linking infant temperament attributes to wakeful resting-state EEG microstates to investigate early self-regulation and emotional reactivity [^10.7273_000007929].

## Computational Neuroimaging and Foundation Models

The integration of foundation models and scalable simulation is transforming structural and functional neuroimaging. BrainFedFM introduces a dual-priority federated pretraining strategy for structural brain MRI, achieving state-of-the-art performance across diverse classification and segmentation tasks without pooling raw images [^2609.27611]. At the mechanistic level, CerebroSim enables scalable whole-brain spiking simulation at the 100-trillion-synapse scale on supercomputers, utilizing delay-aware spike broadcast and sparse synapse storage compression to make biologically constrained human-brain models practical for disorder studies [^2609.27482]. Image reconstruction techniques are also advancing rapidly; Directional Total Variation-Regularized Implicit Neural Representations (DTV-INR) integrate coordinate-driven networks with anisotropic regularizers to achieve continuous super-resolution in clinical brain MRI, circumventing staircasing artifacts [^2609.25429]. Similarly, uncertainty-aware 3D residual wavelet diffusion models address memory bottlenecks in ultra low-field MRI super-resolution, generating per-voxel uncertainty maps that highlight anatomical ambiguity while preserving disease-relevant atrophy [^2609.25319]. Motion correction in magnetic resonance fingerprinting has been enhanced by compact spiral navigators, enabling sub-second motion estimation and improving quantitative map reliability under rapid, unpredictable motion [^2609.25313]. Methodologically, additive nonparametric regression frameworks incorporating Gaussian process priors effectively predict task-based brain activation maps by modeling intricate functional dependencies between structural, resting-state, and task-fMRI data [^2609.26867]. In developmental contexts, deep learning is shifting infant functional neuroimaging from group-level analyses toward reliable, individualized trajectory forecasting [^2609.26688]. Comparative studies using EEG encoding models reveal that vision transformers sustain brain-predictive correspondence at deeper network layers better than CNNs, largely due to preserved patch-token representations [^2609.26512]. Additionally, functional causal discovery via conditional covariance ordering provides a distribution-free approach to estimating brain effective connectivity directed acyclic graphs [^2609.27256].

## Neurodegenerative Disorders and Brain Tumors

Understanding and treating brain pathologies continues to drive methodological innovation. Low-level blast exposure in veterans has been linked to statistically significant increases in depression and mental health symptoms, independent of PTSD or alcohol use, suggesting cumulative overpressure accelerates neurodegenerative or affective decline [^2609.25279]. Automated segmentation of brain metastases, which are often small and heterogeneous, benefits from multi-architecture comparisons; transformer-based models like Primus and residual encoder variants show complementary strengths in aggregate Dice scores versus lesion-wise F1 metrics [^2609.24769]. Unsupervised anomaly detection in medical scans is being reformulated as a Bayesian inverse problem with diffusion priors, jointly inferring pseudo-healthy images and spatial anomaly masks to improve localization on FDG PET and FLAIR MRI [^2609.24265]. In Alzheimer's disease research, the amyloid precursor protein (APP) is increasingly viewed as a universal pro-survival molecule rather than merely a pathological substrate, raising critical questions about the safety of broad-spectrum APP suppression therapies [^10.5281_zenodo.20049820]. Liquid biopsy approaches for glioblastoma multiforme (GBM) are advancing through cfDNA analysis; nucleosome repositioning landscapes and repeat-based cfDNA profiling offer non-invasive diagnostic signatures that mirror tumor-specific chromatin reprogramming [^10.5526_err-00042862][^10.5526_err-00042900]. Spatially resolved single-cell datasets for pan-cancer brain metastases have been released to facilitate microenvironment mapping [^10.5281_zenodo.21816284][^10.5281_zenodo.21782885]. Therapeutic delivery across the blood-brain barrier is being addressed via lipid nanoparticle formulations encapsulating siRNA targeting TERT or loaded with anticancer phytochemicals, demonstrating robust gene silencing and cytotoxic effects in GBM cell lines [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059253][^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059033]. Furthermore, RET signaling pathways have been identified as mediators of estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer brain metastasis [^10.5281_zenodo.19501083], while secretomes and extracellular vesicles from neural crest-derived stem cells show promise in inhibiting GBM proliferation and modulating inflammatory microenvironments [^10.48683_1926.00128572]. Finally, aerobic exercise is proposed to reshape nitric oxide signaling toward adaptive profiles that may enhance mitophagy and provide neuroprotection in Parkinson's disease [^10.4103_mgr.medgasres-d-26-00008].

## Cognitive Neuroscience and Developmental Dynamics

Cognitive and developmental frameworks are being enriched by computational and molecular insights. AI-driven neural surrogate frameworks combine fMRI decoding with constrained latent-space steering to propose and test predicted perceptual effects for cognitive-affective neuromodulation, offering a falsifiable upstream method for designing psychiatric intervention targets [^2609.27729]. The concept of task-based neurons challenges traditional architecture designs, arguing that customized neurons capture task-specific prior knowledge; the TNLearn package facilitates automated construction and training of these networks [^2609.27564]. Theoretical models also explore emotions as intrinsic colored noise in biological networks, demonstrating how decision dynamics and opinion formation shift under varying imitation effects and memory ranges [^2609.25970]. Clinical gait analysis benefits from diagonal segment extraction from dynamic time warping paths, transforming DTW into an interpretable tool for localizing coordination breakdowns in neurodegenerative diseases [^2609.24223]. Cross-cultural and aging studies reveal that category fluency performance is shaped by both gray matter volume variations in semantic control regions and culturally distinct cognitive categorization strategies [^10.48617_etd.1671]. In motor control, brain-based digital twins fuse EEG with kinematic data to model skilled interceptive movements, providing real-time neural efficiency feedback through synthetic data augmentation and closed-loop intervention [^10.17870_bathspa.00017805]. At the molecular level, circular RNAs accumulate in the Drosophila brain at rates corresponding to biological age, serving as stable biomarkers of the aging process and insulin-related pathways [^10.48617_etd.1568]. Systemic metabolism also intersects with brain function, as arteriovenous metabolomics profiling reveals significant organ-specific uptake and release of circulating metabolites, including those relevant to cerebral physiology [^10.5281_zenodo.20652506].

## Neural Signaling and Circuit Mechanisms

Fundamental circuit mechanisms continue to be elucidated through targeted signaling studies. Cholecystokinin (CCK) signaling in the nucleus of the solitary tract (NTS) plays a critical role in satiety and energy balance; NTS-CCK neurons are directly connected to vagal afferents and can release local CCK to activate downstream neurons, with high-fat diets shown to blunt central CCK signaling in males [^10.7273_000008411]. Olfactory system organization is also being redefined, revealing that each olfactory receptor is expressed at a unique mean dorsoventral position in the epithelium, instantiating a stereotyped receptor map that aligns sensory maps in the nose and brain through a coherent gene expression program [^10.5281_zenodo.18941307].
