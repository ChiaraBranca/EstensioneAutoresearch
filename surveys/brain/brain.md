# Living Survey: brain

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **brain**.

## Recent Advances in Brain Research

### Brain Imaging, Segmentation, and Clinical Applications
Recent developments in neuroimaging and medical image analysis have significantly improved the precision of brain tumor segmentation and disease progression modeling. Deep learning frameworks like **NeuroTS-Net** and **De-GAN** have advanced multi-class semantic segmentation of pediatric brain tumors and contrast-enhancing FLAIR synthesis, respectively, outperforming baseline methods in Dice scores and handling domain shifts [^2609.16873][^2609.16755]. Foundation models such as **BrainVLM** integrate multi-modal data to classify WHO brain tumor types with diagnostic uncertainty quantification [^2609.16597], while **GRIN+** addresses machine unlearning for imbalanced medical data to protect patient privacy without compromising rare condition diagnosis [^2609.15571]. Furthermore, **nnU-Net** generalization across heterogeneous brain tumor populations has been rigorously evaluated, highlighting challenges in small tumor volumes [^2609.15524]. In the realm of diffusion MRI, **DTI-SHNet** enables single-to-multi-shell synthesis, preserving downstream diffusion measures [^2609.14312]. Clinical assessment tools have also evolved, with **TractSpLearn** detecting subtle white matter alterations in traumatic brain injury [^2609.15342] and reverse spatio-temporal modeling reconstructing healthier brain states from Alzheimer's disease progression [^2609.14590]. Ex-vivo calf brain models with integrated force sensors now allow high-fidelity quantification of tool-tissue interactions during simulated neurosurgery [^2609.14198], and 4D cerebral microvascular imaging platforms visualize hemodynamic responses in stroke models [^2609.16666].

### EEG, Brain Signal Analysis, and Digital Twins
The analysis of electroencephalography (EEG) and brain signals has seen a surge in foundation models and zero-shot learning capabilities. **METIS** establishes a new paradigm for multi-task brain signal analysis, pretrained on over 70,000 hours of recordings and outperforming generalist models in zero-shot evaluations [^2609.15740]. Similarly, **iMINDBench** provides a standardized benchmark for iEEG neural decoding across institutions, revealing the limits of scaling supervised data [^2609.18104]. Generative decoding has also advanced, with **Mind2Cloud** utilizing two-granularity diffusion decoding to reconstruct 3D point clouds from EEG signals [^2609.13991]. Transformer-based frameworks with spectrogram representations have improved schizophrenia detection from EEG [^2609.14015], while studies on infant EEG microstates link temperament attributes to specific brain topographies [^10.7273_000007929]. On the modeling front, **brain-based digital twins** (BB-DT) fuse EEG with kinematic data for real-time motor imagery feedback [^10.17870_bathspa.00017805], and stochastic models combining Random Neural Networks with Markov-Modulated Poisson Processes aim to improve the fidelity of brain digital twins [^2609.13973].

### Computational Neuroscience and Brain Connectivity
Theoretical and computational approaches continue to unravel brain dynamics and structural fingerprints. **Graphlets** serve as structural fingerprints for complex networks, capturing local-to-mesoscale topology in resting-state functional connectomes and schizophrenia-related alterations [^2609.17445]. Nonparametric correlation estimators solving cubic equations have been applied to analyze dynamic functional connectivity across six frequency bands [^2609.13848]. Theoretical models suggest that moderate neural noise, modeled as temporally correlated fluctuations, is critical for faithful internal simulation of rare events in Bayesian Confidence Propagation Neural Networks (BCPNN) [^2609.18033]. Evolutionary neuro-computational studies challenge the Cognitive Buffer Hypothesis, showing that larger neural networks in dynamic environments often arise from prior static evolution [^2609.15569]. Additionally, spatial codes governing olfactory receptor choice align sensory maps in the nose and brain, revealing a continuously varying transcriptional code for smell [^10.5281_zenodo.18941307][^10.5281_zenodo.18941308], while cholecystokinin signaling in the nucleus of the solitary tract modulates satiety and is blunted by high-fat diets [^10.7273_000008411].

### Neuro-Oncology, Disease Mechanisms, and Metabolism
Research into brain diseases and metabolic profiling continues to expand. Glioblastoma (GBM) remains a focal point, with studies on nucleosome repositioning in cfDNA offering non-invasive liquid biopsy diagnostics [^10.5526_err-00042862][^10.5526_err-00042900]. Lipid-based nanoparticles and LNP-encapsulated siRNA targeting TERT show promise in overcoming the blood-brain barrier for GBM therapy [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059253][^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059033], while secretomes from neural crest-derived stem cells inhibit GBM proliferation and modulate immune responses [^10.48683_1926.00128572]. Pan-cancer brain metastasis atlases provide spatially resolved single-cell data [^10.5281_zenodo.21816284][^10.5281_zenodo.21816285][^10.5281_zenodo.21782885][^10.5281_zenodo.21782884], and RET signaling is identified as a mediator in estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer brain metastasis [^10.5281_zenodo.19501083][^10.5281_zenodo.19501082]. Beyond oncology, comprehensive arteriovenous metabolic profiling reveals cross-organ metabolite exchange, highlighting brain-specific uptake and release patterns [^10.5281_zenodo.20652506][^10.5281_zenodo.20652505]. In neurodegenerative contexts, the amyloid precursor protein (APP) is re-evaluated as a universal survival molecule, critiquing broad-spectrum APP suppression therapies for Alzheimer's [^10.5281_zenodo.20049820], and nitric oxide signaling is linked to aerobic exercise-related neuroprotection in Parkinson's disease via mitophagy modulation [^10.4103_mgr.medgasres-d-26-00008]. Finally, circular RNAs (circRNAs) accumulate in the brain corresponding to biological age, serving as potential biomarkers for aging and insulin-related pathways [^10.48617_etd.1568], while category fluency tasks reveal cultural and age-related gray matter volume associations in semantic processing [^10.48617_etd.1671].
