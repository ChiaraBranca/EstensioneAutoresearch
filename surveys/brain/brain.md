# Living Survey: brain

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **brain**.

## Neuroimaging and MRI Analysis
Recent advancements in large-scale neural recording have enabled the development of comprehensive benchmarks for evaluating transferable neural representations. BrainWideBench addresses fragmented evaluation protocols by utilizing multi-region recordings from 139 mice, systematically testing pretraining methods across decoding, prediction, and anatomical recovery tasks, highlighting the need for models that jointly generalize across behavior, dynamics, and anatomy [^2609.22064]. In medical imaging, unsupervised anomaly detection is being reformulated as a Bayesian inverse problem to infer underlying corruption mechanisms, yielding probabilistic anomaly scores that improve detection on brain MRI benchmarks while estimating pathology intensity [^2609.21800]. High-resolution BOLD fMRI benefits from advanced denoising techniques like Voxel-matching NORDIC, which improves temporal SNR and spatial smoothness by gathering similar non-local voxels based on time-series similarity [^2609.21517]. Ultra-low-field pediatric MRI faces challenges from low SNR and weak anatomical boundaries, addressed by unified dual-stream reconstruction and segmentation networks that enhance volumes and perform subcortical segmentation in a single pipeline [^2609.20562]. Cross-field-strength MRI translation is addressed through conditional latent bridge matching, enabling rapid synthesis of all modality and field-strength combinations [^2609.20341]. Medical image segmentation frameworks are improving cross-slice relation modeling in anisotropic volumetric data via graph-based slice relationship modeling and attention gating, demonstrating superior accuracy on brain MRI datasets [^2609.20088]. Forecasting anatomical changes in neuroimaging is addressed through metadata-conditioned uncertainty-aware synthesis that jointly diffuses baseline scans and follow-up residuals while predicting spatial uncertainty maps [^2609.17169]. Pediatric brain tumor segmentation is improved by three-dimensional encoder-decoder architectures incorporating dual-scale raw-detail streams and adaptive context selection, achieving superior Dice scores on pediatric datasets [^2609.16873]. Brain tumor segmentation under domain shift is addressed through dynamic parameter-tuned conditional GANs that synthesize slice-adaptive FLAIR images, improving segmentation metrics across multiple BraTS datasets [^2609.16755]. Hemodynamic responses in stroke models are being visualized through advanced 4D ultrasound microvascular imaging platforms that enable continuous whole-brain blood-flow signal acquisition during ischemia and reperfusion [^2609.16666].

Recent innovations in foundation models are enhancing diagnostic precision, with BrainVLM integrating uncertainty quantification and radiology report generation to classify WHO brain tumor types across multi-modal data, surpassing neuroradiologist performance in external validation [^2609.16597]. In the realm of privacy-preserving machine learning, GRIN+ addresses the "privacy-efficiency-utility" trilemma in imbalanced medical data by decoupling unlearning-specific knowledge at the parameter level, demonstrating robust performance on brain tumor MRI datasets [^2609.15571]. Segmentation frameworks continue to evolve, as evidenced by nnU-Net generalization assessments across heterogeneous brain tumor populations in BraTS-GoAT 2026, which highlight the impact of volume and component connectivity on segmentation failure [^2609.15524]. For traumatic brain injury, TractSpLearn introduces a shared-manifold learning framework that jointly considers within-group variability and between-group differences, successfully identifying subtle white matter alterations in mild TBI cohorts where conventional methods fall short [^2609.15342]. Diffusion MRI synthesis is advanced by DTI-SHNet, which performs spatially aware volumetric regression in the spherical harmonics domain to generate multi-shell data from single-shell inputs while preserving downstream diffusion measures [^2609.14312]. Low-field MRI reconstruction benefits from physics-informed denoising methods that iteratively apply sub-networks for quantitative parameter estimation and signal estimation, generalizing across field strengths and anatomies without retraining [^2609.12966]. Furthermore, spatial-aware few-shot anomaly detection frameworks like Spatial-FAD combine vision foundation model priors with CLIP semantics to improve lesion localization in brain MRI, significantly boosting Dice scores in low-shot scenarios [^2609.12454]. Computed tomography also sees advancements, with spectral CT material decomposition proving more sensitive than attenuation-based methods for tracking progression in primary familial brain calcification [^https:__openalex.org_W7212340133].

## Brain-Computer Interfaces and Neural Decoding
Proactive assistance systems for cognitive impairment are being enhanced through multimodal sensing. PV-Care integrates wearable EEG with visual environmental perception to detect brain states like learning and memory recall, actively initiating context-aware voice assistance via LLMs, demonstrating high accuracy in real-time cognitive state recognition [^2609.22024]. Hybrid brain-computer interfaces are advancing through low-latency cross-modal generation. Bio-MF introduces a latent-free one-step MeanFlow framework for EEG-conditioned fNIRS generation, achieving an 857x speedup over iterative models while preserving task-relevant hemodynamic structure for real-time motor-imagery decoding [^2609.20904]. Deep learning frameworks are being integrated with signal processing to automatically distinguish resting and cognitive states from EEG records. A CNN-GRU architecture combined with time-frequency analysis achieves high classification accuracy for mathematical, memory, and music tasks, demonstrating the efficacy of automated feature extraction from intricate brain activity [^2609.20467]. General-purpose neural decoding foundation models require robust evaluation protocols. iMINDBench provides a standardized benchmark for intracranial EEG across multiple institutions and naturalistic tasks, revealing that scaling supervised data yields limited gains over within-session training and highlighting the need for improved preprocessing baselines [^2609.18104]. Brain-computer interfaces are being integrated with kinematic data to develop brain-based digital twins for skilled motor imagery. This framework fuses consumer-grade EEG with motion capture to model, predict, and provide real-time feedback on interceptive actions, demonstrating feasibility for rehabilitation and training technologies [^10.17870_bathspa.00017805]. Infant temperament is being correlated with global brain activity patterns measured via EEG microstates, with hypotheses proposing electrophysiological markers for self-regulation and reactivity domains in early development [^10.7273_000007929].

Evaluating the alignment between biological and artificial systems remains a priority, with studies testing two-state access mechanisms in human EEG reproduction and language model simulations to probe global workspace theories [^2609.22300]. Foundation models for brain signals are scaling rapidly; METIS utilizes a unified language-signal alignment framework pretrained on over 70,000 hours of recordings, achieving superior zero-shot and few-shot generalization across diverse datasets without fine-tuning [^2609.15740]. Visual reconstruction from neural activity is advancing through Brain-to-Image generation using GANs, which demonstrates above-chance coarse semantic decoding under repetition-averaged protocols [^2609.22282]. Psychiatric diagnostics are being augmented by transformer frameworks that convert preprocessed EEG into spectrograms for schizophrenia detection, achieving high AUC-ROC scores while preventing subject-related data leakage [^2609.14015]. 3D object reconstruction from brain signals is addressed by Mind2Cloud, a two-granularity diffusion decoding framework that integrates global Transformer and local Point-Voxel CNN branches to adapt to the coarse-to-fine trajectory of denoising [^2609.13991]. Network analysis of EEG data is improved by assumption-lean inference frameworks for spectral differential network analysis, establishing asymptotic normality for de-biased D-trace estimators to study condition-dependent connectivity changes [^2609.13609]. Sample efficiency in neural interfaces is tackled through self-supervised pretraining methods like MAPA, which uses anatomical and relative positional encodings to learn transferable iEEG representations, drastically reducing labeled trial requirements for cross-subject decoding [^2609.13507]. Multimodal decoding strategies like FRIST leverage fMRI-informed spectral projections to guide residual refinement of EEG predictions, significantly improving individual-finger BCI decoding accuracy [^2609.12298]. Additionally, consumer-grade EEG classification for human activities is optimized using Random Forest algorithms, effectively distinguishing real and imagery motor activities despite interpersonal signal variability [^2609.11695]. Clinical neurostimulation is also being refined, with research investigating whether intraoperative therapeutic window data can predict chronic contact selection in deep brain stimulation, aiming to optimize long-term electrode placement [^https:__openalex.org_W7164787306].

## Neurological Disorders and Brain Cancer Therapeutics
Brain disorder classification from neuroimaging data is advanced by adaptive multi-scale spatial hypergraph networks that integrate hypergraph attention and spatial-channel mechanisms to model higher-order dependencies, achieving state-of-the-art performance across autism, depression, and Alzheimer's benchmark datasets [^2609.19755]. Therapeutic strategies for Alzheimer's disease are being critically re-evaluated regarding amyloid precursor protein (APP) suppression. Analyses argue that APP functions as a universal pro-survival molecule deployed during catastrophic neural insults, suggesting that broad-spectrum suppression via RNA interference risks disarming the brain's intrinsic repair mechanisms [^10.5281_zenodo.20049820]. Liquid biopsy approaches for glioblastoma diagnostics are advancing through cell-free DNA analysis. Research on nucleosome positioning in paired tumor and normal tissues highlights specific transcription factor binding sites as potential biomarkers, offering non-invasive alternatives to invasive tissue biopsies for early detection [^10.5526_err-00042862]. Genomic repetitive elements in cell-free DNA are being leveraged for pan-cancer and tissue-specific diagnostics. Machine learning models trained on nucleosome signatures at DNA repeats successfully discriminate healthy individuals from glioblastoma patients, establishing a foundation for repeat-based cfDNA profiling in early cancer detection [^10.5526_err-00042900]. Spatially resolved single-cell atlases are being constructed to map pan-cancer metastatic brain tumors, with new data releases providing 10x Visium datasets for analysis [^10.5281_zenodo.21816284]. Targeted gene silencing strategies for glioblastoma are being enhanced through lipid nanoparticle delivery systems. LNP-encapsulated siRNA targeting TERT demonstrates robust knockdown of oncogenic transcripts, significantly reducing cell viability and offering a promising approach to overcome blood-brain barrier penetration challenges in brain cancer therapy [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059253]. Molecular signaling pathways driving breast cancer metastasis to the brain are being investigated, with code repositories released for studies on RET signaling as a mediator in estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer brain metastasis [^10.5281_zenodo.19501083]. Extracellular vesicles derived from neural crest stem cells are being explored as therapeutic agents for glioblastoma, inhibiting tumor proliferation and migration by modulating NF-κB and IRF3 pathways [^10.48683_1926.00128572]. Novel lipid-based nanoparticle formulations are being developed to enhance drug delivery across the blood-brain barrier for glioblastoma treatment, showing promise for targeted brain tumor therapy [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059033]. Mitochondrial quality control in Parkinson's disease is being linked to aerobic exercise interventions, proposing a context-dependent framework where exercise reshapes nitric oxide signaling toward adaptive profiles that influence mitochondrial biogenesis and mitophagy initiation [^10.4103_mgr.medgasres-d-26-00008].

Disease progression modeling is being inverted to address incubation periods in progressive disorders; reverse spatio-temporal modeling using Neural ODEs successfully reconstructs unobserved healthier brain anatomy from later diseased states in Alzheimer's cohorts [^2609.14590]. Cognitive aging research highlights resting-state EEG markers, particularly alpha oscillations and aperiodic activity, as sensitive biomarkers for sustained attention and memory decline, offering pathways to detect subtle neural plasticity from lifestyle interventions [^10.25907_00976]. The microbiota-gut-brain axis in Parkinson's disease is further elucidated through preclinical models demonstrating bidirectional pathology transmission, with TLR4 inhibition showing therapeutic potential for motor and GI symptoms [^https:__openalex.org_W7213566724]. Genetic epilepsy syndromes are being characterized through case studies of bilateral open-lip schizencephaly complicated by West syndrome, identifying de novo PAFAH1B1 frameshift variants as pathogenic and emphasizing the need for early genetic evaluation [^10.3760_cma.j.cn511374-20260302-00092]. Nanotechnology advances glioblastoma treatment through PEGylated chitosan-gold nanoparticles engineered for radiosensitization and targeted siRNA delivery, enhancing therapeutic efficacy across the blood-brain barrier [^https:__openalex.org_W7204677537]. Neuroinflammation in Alzheimer's disease is mitigated by engineered mesenchymal stem cell-derived extracellular vesicles overexpressing miR-146a, which suppress the NR4A3–NF-κB axis to reduce amyloid-β, tau phosphorylation, and cognitive decline in murine models [^10.4103_nrr.nrr-d-25-00404]. Finally, large-scale visualization techniques for α-synuclein oligomers in Parkinson's disease brain tissue provide critical insights into pathological aggregation patterns, supporting the development of targeted diagnostic and therapeutic strategies [^10.5281_zenodo.18548364].

## Neural Mechanisms, Connectomics, and Signaling
Artificial neural networks are being re-evaluated through biological lenses, with proposals to incorporate neuromodulation principles. Position papers argue that modeling neurotransmitter signaling as a third axis of learning, alongside activity and plasticity, could significantly enhance the adaptability and long-term stability of artificial systems [^2609.20083]. Bayesian neural networks are being utilized to study how the brain constructs accurate internal models from limited experience. Research indicates that moderate neural noise is critical for faithful internal simulation of rare events, restoring marginal and conditional occurrence frequencies that deterministic replay fails to capture [^2609.18033]. Complex network analysis of resting-state functional connectomes is advanced through graphlet fingerprints that capture local-to-mesotopology differences with high sensitivity, highlighting spatially localized connectivity alterations in schizophrenia [^2609.17445]. Transcranial ultrasound stimulation safety and efficacy are improved through optimized acoustic lens design, with research on phase unwrapping for bifocal lenses demonstrating significant improvements in spatial precision and reduction of out-of-target energy deposition across human skull models [^2609.18495]. Cognitive performance in category fluency tasks is linked to structural brain integrity and cultural factors, revealing age-related differences in gray matter volumes in prefrontal and temporal regions that influence cognitive strategies across different cultural backgrounds [^10.48617_etd.1671]. The organization of sensory maps in the brain is being clarified through spatial coding mechanisms, revealing that olfactory receptor choice follows a coherent dorsoventral transcriptional code that aligns receptor maps in the nose with precise topographical organization in the brain [^10.5281_zenodo.18941307]. Gut-brain signaling mechanisms are being elucidated through vagal afferent pathways, demonstrating that local CCK release modulates satiety and food intake, with high-fat diets blunting central CCK signaling and linking peripheral metabolism to central neural regulation [^10.7273_000008411]. Inter-organ metabolite exchange is being quantified through arteriovenous profiling across human organs, revealing significant brain-specific uptake and release patterns that provide a quantitative foundation for understanding cross-organ metabolic interactions [^10.5281_zenodo.20652506].

Biologically-inspired computing is advancing through scaled hippocampus-inspired spiking neural networks demonstrated on neuromorphic memristive hardware, utilizing a network downscaling methodology to preserve biologically-realistic resting-state dynamics with high energy efficiency [^2609.16429]. Evolutionary neuroscience challenges strict cognitive buffer hypotheses by showing that larger neural networks in dynamic environments primarily arise from prior static evolution, highlighting the role of evolutionary history in brain size adaptation [^2609.15569]. Digital twin fidelity is improved by a new stochastic model combining Random Neural Networks and Markov-Modulated Poisson Processes, mapped to Discrete Event System Specifications for executable whole-brain simulation [^2609.13973]. Functional connectivity analysis benefits from a novel nonparametric correlation estimator based on cubic equations, enabling time-varying correlation estimation and correcting boundary effects in dynamic brain network studies [^2609.13848]. Foundation models for brain dynamics are evaluated through perturbational validity frameworks, demonstrating that input-driven pretraining yields lower errors in recovering controlled flow and landscape geometry compared to passive prediction models [^2609.12710]. Cortical mapping of sensory stimuli is refined using Bayesian semiparametric hidden Markov random partition fields, which promote sparsity in factor influence partitions and capture subtle local variations across the brain's cortical graph [^2609.13319].
