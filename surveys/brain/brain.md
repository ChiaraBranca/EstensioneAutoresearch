# Living Survey: brain

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **brain**.

## Recent Advances in Brain Research

### Brain Imaging, Segmentation, and Clinical Applications
Recent developments in neuroimaging and medical image analysis have significantly improved the precision of brain tumor segmentation and disease progression modeling. Deep learning frameworks like **NeuroTS-Net** and **De-GAN** have advanced multi-class semantic segmentation of pediatric brain tumors and contrast-enhancing FLAIR synthesis, respectively, outperforming baseline methods in Dice scores and handling domain shifts [^2609.16873][^2609.16755]. Foundation models such as **BrainVLM** integrate multi-modal data to classify WHO brain tumor types with diagnostic uncertainty quantification [^2609.16597], while **GRIN+** addresses machine unlearning for imbalanced medical data to protect patient privacy without compromising rare condition diagnosis [^2609.15571]. Furthermore, **nnU-Net** generalization across heterogeneous brain tumor populations has been rigorously evaluated, highlighting challenges in small tumor volumes [^2609.15524]. In the realm of diffusion MRI, **DTI-SHNet** enables single-to-multi-shell synthesis, preserving downstream diffusion measures [^2609.14312]. Clinical assessment tools have also evolved, with **TractSpLearn** detecting subtle white matter alterations in traumatic brain injury [^2609.15342] and reverse spatio-temporal modeling reconstructing healthier brain states from Alzheimer's disease progression [^2609.14590]. Ex-vivo calf brain models with integrated force sensors now allow high-fidelity quantification of tool-tissue interactions during simulated neurosurgery [^2609.14198], and 4D cerebral microvascular imaging platforms visualize hemodynamic responses in stroke models [^2609.16666].

Furthermore, accelerated MRI sequences have been enhanced through implicit neural representation reconstruction, enabling high-quality 3D MPnRAGE scans in clinically feasible times [^2609.18589]. Physics-informed deep learning methods now allow for robust denoising and quantitative parameter map reconstruction in low-field MRI, generalizing across different field strengths and anatomies without retraining [^2609.12966]. In the domain of anomaly detection, frameworks like Spatial-FAD combine vision foundation model priors with CLIP semantics to improve lesion localization in brain MRI, significantly boosting segmentation performance in few-shot scenarios [^2609.12454]. Longitudinal brain aging modeling has also advanced with Brain-PACE, a Siamese MRI framework that directly estimates the pace of structural brain aging, showing strong associations with cognitive impairment and tau burden [^2609.11378]. On the computational side, deep registration pipelines now align complex brain volumes in a single forward pass, drastically reducing computation time while preserving high spatial resolution [^2609.11240][^2609.10789]. Additionally, adversarial robustness in medical hyperspectral imaging has been probed using brain datasets, revealing critical vulnerabilities in deep learning models that rely on spectral-spatial dependencies [^10.1007_s44267-026-00129-x].

### EEG, Brain Signal Analysis, and Digital Twins
The analysis of electroencephalography (EEG) and brain signals has seen a surge in foundation models and zero-shot learning capabilities. **METIS** establishes a new paradigm for multi-task brain signal analysis, pretrained on over 70,000 hours of recordings and outperforming generalist models in zero-shot evaluations [^2609.15740]. Similarly, **iMINDBench** provides a standardized benchmark for iEEG neural decoding across institutions, revealing the limits of scaling supervised data [^2609.18104]. Generative decoding has also advanced, with **Mind2Cloud** utilizing two-granularity diffusion decoding to reconstruct 3D point clouds from EEG signals [^2609.13991]. Transformer-based frameworks with spectrogram representations have improved schizophrenia detection from EEG [^2609.14015], while studies on infant EEG microstates link temperament attributes to specific brain topographies [^10.7273_000007929]. On the modeling front, **brain-based digital twins** (BB-DT) fuse EEG with kinematic data for real-time motor imagery feedback [^10.17870_bathspa.00017805], and stochastic models combining Random Neural Networks with Markov-Modulated Poisson Processes aim to improve the fidelity of brain digital twins [^2609.13973].

Advancements in EEG and neural signal processing continue to push the boundaries of zero-shot learning and multimodal integration. The MAPA framework leverages masked autoencoders with anatomical and positional encodings to learn transferable neural representations from iEEG recordings, drastically reducing the labeled data required for cross-subject decoding [^2609.13507]. Similarly, FRIST utilizes fMRI-derived class geometry to guide the residual refinement of EEG predictions, improving individual-finger BCI decoding without requiring paired trials at inference [^2609.12298]. Generative and classification models have also been optimized, with Random Forest algorithms proving effective for classifying real and imagery motor activities from consumer-grade EEG [^2609.11695], and CoMA-DiT employing bidirectional cross-modal diffusion transformers to augment multimodal brain state decoding [^2609.11341]. However, pipeline design remains complex, as demonstrated by findings that component anti-synergy can occur in P300 spellers, where adding all available filters and priors does not universally improve performance [^2609.10961]. Spectral differential network analysis methods have further been developed to infer high-dimensional time series changes in EEG data, providing robust window size selection and asymptotic normality for de-biased estimators [^2609.13609].

### Computational Neuroscience and Brain Connectivity
Theoretical and computational approaches continue to unravel brain dynamics and structural fingerprints. **Graphlets** serve as structural fingerprints for complex networks, capturing local-to-mesoscale topology in resting-state functional connectomes and schizophrenia-related alterations [^2609.17445]. Nonparametric correlation estimators solving cubic equations have been applied to analyze dynamic functional connectivity across six frequency bands [^2609.13848]. Theoretical models suggest that moderate neural noise, modeled as temporally correlated fluctuations, is critical for faithful internal simulation of rare events in Bayesian Confidence Propagation Neural Networks (BCPNN) [^2609.18033]. Evolutionary neuro-computational studies challenge the Cognitive Buffer Hypothesis, showing that larger neural networks in dynamic environments often arise from prior static evolution [^2609.15569]. Additionally, spatial codes governing olfactory receptor choice align sensory maps in the nose and brain, revealing a continuously varying transcriptional code for smell [^10.5281_zenodo.18941307][^10.5281_zenodo.18941308], while cholecystokinin signaling in the nucleus of the solitary tract modulates satiety and is blunted by high-fat diets [^10.7273_000008411].

Theoretical and computational neuroscience continues to explore brain dynamics, connectivity, and the intersection of biological networks with artificial intelligence. Foundation models for brain recordings are now being evaluated for perturbational validity, ensuring they preserve conditional distributions of future trajectories under controlled inputs [^2609.12710]. Bayesian hidden Markov random fields have been introduced to partition cortical influence into collapsed states, enabling highly detailed local influence maps of fingertip tapping responses [^2609.13319]. The spatiotemporal organization of neuronal avalanches is now systematically analyzed using pyAvalanches, which computes transition matrices to map activity propagation across EEG recordings [^2609.11530]. For MEG data, a regression-based framework utilizing radial basis functions and gradient boosting provides spatially resolved cortical activation estimates, overcoming the limitations of traditional cluster-based permutation tests [^2609.11278]. The intersection of neuroscience and AI is further highlighted by the Platonic brain bridge hypothesis, which posits that human brain networks can serve as architectural priors for multimodal large language models [^2609.10947]. Cortical information transfer studies using permutation transfer entropy reveal that hemispherical network dynamics are conserved across human handedness during motor execution [^2609.10870]. Finally, dual-contrastive learning frameworks like STAMGC are being applied to spatial transcriptomics to identify finer brain structures and reduce noise in domain identification [^10.1101_gr.281999.126].

### Neuro-Oncology, Disease Mechanisms, and Metabolism
Research into brain diseases and metabolic profiling continues to expand. Glioblastoma (GBM) remains a focal point, with studies on nucleosome repositioning in cfDNA offering non-invasive liquid biopsy diagnostics [^10.5526_err-00042862][^10.5526_err-00042900]. Lipid-based nanoparticles and LNP-encapsulated siRNA targeting TERT show promise in overcoming the blood-brain barrier for GBM therapy [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059253][^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059033], while secretomes from neural crest-derived stem cells inhibit GBM proliferation and modulate immune responses [^10.48683_1926.00128572]. Pan-cancer brain metastasis atlases provide spatially resolved single-cell data [^10.5281_zenodo.21816284][^10.5281_zenodo.21816285][^10.5281_zenodo.21782885][^10.5281_zenodo.21782884], and RET signaling is identified as a mediator in estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer brain metastasis [^10.5281_zenodo.19501083][^10.5281_zenodo.19501082]. Beyond oncology, comprehensive arteriovenous metabolic profiling reveals cross-organ metabolite exchange, highlighting brain-specific uptake and release patterns [^10.5281_zenodo.20652506][^10.5281_zenodo.20652505]. In neurodegenerative contexts, the amyloid precursor protein (APP) is re-evaluated as a universal survival molecule, critiquing broad-spectrum APP suppression therapies for Alzheimer's [^10.5281_zenodo.20049820], and nitric oxide signaling is linked to aerobic exercise-related neuroprotection in Parkinson's disease via mitophagy modulation [^10.4103_mgr.medgasres-d-26-00008]. Finally, circular RNAs (circRNAs) accumulate in the brain corresponding to biological age, serving as potential biomarkers for aging and insulin-related pathways [^10.48617_etd.1568], while category fluency tasks reveal cultural and age-related gray matter volume associations in semantic processing [^10.48617_etd.1671].

Neuro-oncology and disease mechanism research has seen significant strides in metastasis detection, therapeutic delivery, and pathological classification. Deep learning ensembles based on nnU-Net have been optimized for pre- and post-treatment brain metastases segmentation, with rigorous out-of-fold audits confirming the robustness of post-processing cascades [^2609.11477]. Scale-aware 3D deep learning frameworks combining different spatial fields of view have improved the precision-false-positive trade-off in brain metastasis detection [^2609.10825]. Stereotactic radiosurgery for thalamic metastases demonstrates high local control rates, supporting its role as a safe and effective treatment option [^10.1227_neuprac.0000000000000284], while frailty indices show limited incremental value in predicting outcomes for vestibular schwannoma surgeries compared to tumor characteristics [^10.1227_neuprac.0000000000000289]. Cellular atlases of breast cancer brain metastases reveal prognostically distinct immune landscapes, highlighting the need for tailored immunotherapies [^10.5281_zenodo.17723783][^10.5281_zenodo.17723784]. Therapeutic delivery mechanisms are advancing with medical microbubbles, which show potential for bridging the blood-brain barrier in gene therapy and cancer treatment [^10.5281_zenodo.19676283][^10.5281_zenodo.19676282]. In neurodegeneration and injury, differential DNA methylation in blood is linked to cerebrospinal fluid biomarkers of Alzheimer's disease [^10.1038_s41380-026-03662-9], and artemisinin has been shown to alleviate hippocampal neuronal apoptosis and cognitive dysfunction following cardiac arrest [^10.36721_pjps.2026.39.12.334.1]. Qualitative studies also explore the potential of psychedelics in traumatic brain injury recovery, noting reductions in trauma-related distress and perceived cognitive improvements [^10.3389_fneur.2026.1880026]. Finally, delayed diagnosis remains a critical challenge in central nervous system intravascular large B-cell lymphoma, where brain biopsy is often required after prolonged neurological deterioration [^10.52662_nf.2026.00276].
