# Living Survey: brain

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **brain**.

## Neuro-Oncology and Therapeutic Innovations

Recent advances in brain tumor segmentation and diagnosis have leveraged advanced deep learning architectures. NeuroTS-Net [^2609.16873] and DE-GAN [^2609.16755] introduce novel 3D CNN and conditional GAN frameworks that significantly improve multi-class semantic segmentation of pediatric brain tumors and enhance tumor-core delineation in FLAIR images. Complementing these, BrainVLM [^2609.16597] integrates vision-language modeling with uncertainty quantification to classify WHO brain tumor types and generate radiology reports, while GRIN+ [^2609.15571] addresses machine unlearning for imbalanced medical data to preserve privacy without sacrificing diagnostic accuracy. Generalization across heterogeneous populations is further evaluated in BraTS-GoAT 2026 [^2609.15524], highlighting the challenges of small enhancing tumor volumes. Therapeutic delivery mechanisms are also advancing, with lipid nanoparticle formulations targeting TERT [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059253] and [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059033] showing promise in overcoming the blood-brain barrier for glioblastoma. Furthermore, liquid biopsy approaches utilizing cell-free DNA nucleosome positioning [^10.5526_err-00042862] and [^10.5526_err-00042900] offer non-invasive diagnostic potential, while single-cell spatial atlases [^10.5281_zenodo.21816284], [^10.5281_zenodo.21816285], [^10.5281_zenodo.21782885], [^10.5281_zenodo.21782884] map pan-cancer metastatic brain tumors. Secretome and extracellular vesicle modulation from neural crest stem cells [^10.48683_1926.00128572] and RET signaling pathways in breast cancer brain metastasis [^10.5281_zenodo.19501083], [^10.5281_zenodo.19501082] provide new avenues for understanding tumor microenvironments and metastatic mechanisms.

## Neuroimaging and Signal Processing

The analysis of brain signals and imaging modalities continues to evolve with robust benchmarks and foundation models. iMINDBench [^2609.18104] establishes a standardized framework for iEEG neural decoding across institutions, revealing the limits of scaling supervised data. For broader brain signal analysis, METIS [^2609.15740] introduces a language-guided multimodal foundation model pretrained on over 70,000 hours of recordings, achieving state-of-the-art zero-shot performance. Medical image synthesis and uncertainty quantification are addressed by MUMINS [^2609.17169], which jointly diffuses baseline scans and follow-up residuals to predict anatomical changes. Diffusion MRI synthesis is improved via DTI-SHNet [^2609.14312], which performs spatially aware volumetric regression to generate multi-shell data from single-shell inputs. Cerebral hemodynamics are visualized in real-time using a 4D microvascular imaging platform for stroke models [^2609.16666], while TractSpLearn [^2609.15342] detects subtle white matter alterations in traumatic brain injury through shared-manifold learning. Schizophrenia detection benefits from transformer-based EEG spectrogram analysis [^2609.14015], and 3D object reconstruction from brain signals is advanced by Mind2Cloud [^2609.13991], which employs two-granularity diffusion decoding. Dynamic functional connectivity is further explored using a novel nonparametric correlation estimator [^2609.13848]. Cognitive and structural brain changes are linked to age and culture in studies examining gray matter volumes [^10.48617_etd.1671], while infant temperament is correlated with EEG microstates [^10.7273_000007929]. Cross-organ metabolic profiling also highlights specific brain uptake and release patterns [^10.5281_zenodo.20652506], [^10.5281_zenodo.20652505].

## Computational Neuroscience and Brain Modeling

Theoretical and computational models are providing deeper insights into brain dynamics, digital twins, and evolutionary adaptations. Transcranial holography for deep-brain stimulation is optimized through phase unwrapping techniques [^2609.18495], while moderate neural noise is shown to enable accurate internal simulation of rare events in Bayesian networks [^2609.18033]. Graphlet-based structural fingerprints [^2609.17445] outperform classical measures in analyzing resting-state connectomes. Hippocampus-inspired spiking neural networks are successfully mapped to neuromorphic memristive hardware [^2609.16429], and dichoptic foveation models [^2609.16385] explain interocular visual perception in VR. Evolutionary neurobiology suggests that large brains may evolve in stable environments before colonizing dynamic ones [^2609.15569]. Disease progression modeling is inverted to reconstruct healthier brain states from diseased MRIs in Alzheimer's research [^2609.14590]. Neurosurgical training is enhanced by ex-vivo calf brain models with integrated force sensors [^2609.14198]. Faithful stochastic models for brain digital twins [^2609.13973] propose new computational frameworks, while brain-based digital twins for motor imagery [^10.17870_bathspa.00017805] integrate EEG and kinematics for real-time feedback. At the molecular level, circRNAs serve as biomarkers for biological aging in the brain [^10.48617_etd.1568], and spatial codes governing olfactory receptor choice align sensory maps in the nose and brain [^10.5281_zenodo.18941307], [^10.5281_zenodo.18941308]. Central vagal afferent signaling in the nucleus of the solitary tract [^10.7273_000008411] and nitric oxide signaling in Parkinson's disease [^10.4103_mgr.medgasres-d-26-00008] further elucidate neurochemical pathways. The amyloid precursor protein is re-evaluated as a universal survival molecule rather than solely a pathological target in Alzheimer's [^10.5281_zenodo.20049820].
