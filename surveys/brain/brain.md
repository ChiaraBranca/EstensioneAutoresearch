# Living Survey: brain

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **brain**.

## Neuroimaging and MRI Analysis
Recent advancements in large-scale neural recording have enabled the development of comprehensive benchmarks for evaluating transferable neural representations. BrainWideBench addresses fragmented evaluation protocols by utilizing multi-region recordings from 139 mice, systematically testing pretraining methods across decoding, prediction, and anatomical recovery tasks, highlighting the need for models that jointly generalize across behavior, dynamics, and anatomy [^2609.22064]. In medical imaging, unsupervised anomaly detection is being reformulated as a Bayesian inverse problem to infer underlying corruption mechanisms, yielding probabilistic anomaly scores that improve detection on brain MRI benchmarks while estimating pathology intensity [^2609.21800]. High-resolution BOLD fMRI benefits from advanced denoising techniques like Voxel-matching NORDIC, which improves temporal SNR and spatial smoothness by gathering similar non-local voxels based on time-series similarity [^2609.21517]. Ultra-low-field pediatric MRI faces challenges from low SNR and weak anatomical boundaries, addressed by unified dual-stream reconstruction and segmentation networks that enhance volumes and perform subcortical segmentation in a single pipeline [^2609.20562]. Cross-field-strength MRI translation is addressed through conditional latent bridge matching, enabling rapid synthesis of all modality and field-strength combinations [^2609.20341]. Medical image segmentation frameworks are improving cross-slice relation modeling in anisotropic volumetric data via graph-based slice relationship modeling and attention gating, demonstrating superior accuracy on brain MRI datasets [^2609.20088]. Forecasting anatomical changes in neuroimaging is addressed through metadata-conditioned uncertainty-aware synthesis that jointly diffuses baseline scans and follow-up residuals while predicting spatial uncertainty maps [^2609.17169]. Pediatric brain tumor segmentation is improved by three-dimensional encoder-decoder architectures incorporating dual-scale raw-detail streams and adaptive context selection, achieving superior Dice scores on pediatric datasets [^2609.16873]. Brain tumor segmentation under domain shift is addressed through dynamic parameter-tuned conditional GANs that synthesize slice-adaptive FLAIR images, improving segmentation metrics across multiple BraTS datasets [^2609.16755]. Hemodynamic responses in stroke models are being visualized through advanced 4D ultrasound microvascular imaging platforms that enable continuous whole-brain blood-flow signal acquisition during ischemia and reperfusion [^2609.16666].

Recent innovations in foundation models are enhancing diagnostic precision, with BrainVLM integrating uncertainty quantification and radiology report generation to classify WHO brain tumor types across multi-modal data, surpassing neuroradiologist performance in external validation [^2609.16597]. In the realm of privacy-preserving machine learning, GRIN+ addresses the "privacy-efficiency-utility" trilemma in imbalanced medical data by decoupling unlearning-specific knowledge at the parameter level, demonstrating robust performance on brain tumor MRI datasets [^2609.15571]. Segmentation frameworks continue to evolve, as evidenced by nnU-Net generalization assessments across heterogeneous brain tumor populations in BraTS-GoAT 2026, which highlight the impact of volume and component connectivity on segmentation failure [^2609.15524]. For traumatic brain injury, TractSpLearn introduces a shared-manifold learning framework that jointly considers within-group variability and between-group differences, successfully identifying subtle white matter alterations in mild TBI cohorts where conventional methods fall short [^2609.15342]. Diffusion MRI synthesis is advanced by DTI-SHNet, which performs spatially aware volumetric regression in the spherical harmonics domain to generate multi-shell data from single-shell inputs while preserving downstream diffusion measures [^2609.14312]. Low-field MRI reconstruction benefits from physics-informed denoising methods that iteratively apply sub-networks for quantitative parameter estimation and signal estimation, generalizing across field strengths and anatomies without retraining [^2609.12966]. Furthermore, spatial-aware few-shot anomaly detection frameworks like Spatial-FAD combine vision foundation model priors with CLIP semantics to improve lesion localization in brain MRI, significantly boosting Dice scores in low-shot scenarios [^2609.12454]. Computed tomography also sees advancements, with spectral CT material decomposition proving more sensitive than attenuation-based methods for tracking progression in primary familial brain calcification [^https:__openalex.org_W7212340133].

Recent advancements in brain metastasis segmentation leverage ensemble deep learning architectures. A pragmatic pipeline utilizing a 5-fold nnU-Net ResEnc-L ensemble combined with a rule-based post-processing cascade achieves high lesion-wise Dice similarity coefficients, with rigorous out-of-fold audits confirming robust improvements for specific stages, while noting that some leaderboard gains did not reproduce out-of-fold [^2609.11477]. Similarly, scale-aware 3D deep learning frameworks improve metastasis detection by fusing probability maps from independently trained $96^3$ and $64^3$ U-Nets, demonstrating that cross-field-of-view fusion significantly reduces false positives while preserving spatial context [^2609.10825]. Longitudinal brain acceleration modeling is advanced by Brain-PACE, a deep Siamese MRI framework that directly estimates the pace of structural brain ageing from paired T1-weighted scans, outperforming indirect cross-sectional methods and revealing stronger associations with tau burden and cognitive impairment [^2609.11378]. In cortical activation analysis, a regression-based framework utilizing Wendland radial basis functions and model-based gradient boosting provides spatially resolved effect estimates on the cortical surface, mitigating multiple testing burdens and recovering localized effects in MEG data that cluster-based methods miss [^2609.11278]. High-resolution brain tissue imaging benefits from LUCID, a laminography framework combining multi-view diffusion priors with projection-domain data consistency to restore missing Fourier components and recover unmeasured structural information in nanoscale connectomics [^2609.10456]. Drosophila larval brain registration is accelerated through a trained deep pipeline that deformably aligns volumes to a reference template in a single forward pass, achieving superior anatomical landmark-local mutual information and operating orders of magnitude faster than classical methods [^2609.11240]. Furthermore, fMRI studies on reading networks reveal that while the ventral temporal text-selective regions can detect multiple words simultaneously, lexical access suffers interference during concurrent presentation, as evidenced by attenuated sensitivity to lexical frequencies [^10.1162_jocn.a.2624]. Memory precision during retrieval is also tracked by eye movements, with fixation patterns predicting successful location recall and correlating with hippocampal BOLD activity independently [^10.1162_jocn.a.2627].

Recent methodological advances in MRI registration and parcellation are enhancing clinical precision. For trigeminal neuralgia, ROI-centered neurovascular benchmarks address the limitations of whole-brain registration by focusing on local vessel-aware metrics and contrast sensitivity, revealing that deformable refinement offers only marginal benefits over affine alignment [^2609.08805]. In brain parcellation, Spatial Feature-wise Linear Modulation (SpFiLM) introduces voxel-wise scale and shift conditioning to handle contrast-agent variations, significantly improving Dice scores on paired T1w and T1ce MRI cohorts [^2609.07718]. Topological data analysis is also being applied to neuroimaging; persistent homology frameworks model Betti curves as count-valued functional responses to capture longitudinal structural variation in dementia, while landmark embeddings for persistence diagrams provide finite-sample power guarantees for resting-state connectivity analysis in autism cohorts [^2609.07683][^2609.07691]. Furthermore, structure-adaptive e-value filters translate voxel-level evidence into regional conclusions for Alzheimer's disease, controlling false discovery rates under arbitrary interregional dependence [^2609.07246]. Foundation models for structural MRI are being evaluated against traditional anatomical features, revealing that CNNs and ViTs implicitly capture relevant anatomy while bypassing explicit extraction, leading to novel pretraining methods like Anatomy Segmentation Pretraining [^2609.06849]. Unsupervised anomaly detection is being reformulated using diffusion priors as a Bayesian inverse problem, yielding probabilistic scores that improve pathology intensity estimation [^https:__openalex.org_W7213384286]. Additionally, quantitative MRI is being leveraged to estimate SV2A PET-derived synaptic density through data-driven methods, bridging molecular and structural imaging modalities [^https:__openalex.org_W7162725668]. Finally, training data attribution methods are being applied to normative age models, allowing direct attribution of disease-related effect sizes to individual training samples and revealing subclinical biomarkers missed by standard exclusion criteria [^2609.07729].

Recent methodological advances in Alzheimer's disease research leverage transfer learning to overcome data scarcity. A compact 3D CNN pretrained for brain-age prediction serves as a reusable foundation model for downstream neuroimaging tasks, adapting to classification and volume prediction with only ~1% additional trainable parameters via Low-Rank Adaptation, demonstrating strong generalizability across cohorts without retraining [^2609.05400]. In medical image inpainting, structural similarity is being enhanced through post-processing ensembles. An SSIM-aligned residual refiner improves synthesized regions in BraTS benchmarks by augmenting $\ell_1$ losses with structural terms, yielding consistent gains without large-scale retraining [^2609.03981]. Similarly, region-aware rectified flows restrict stochastic interpolation to masked areas, allowing observed voxels to provide patient-specific anatomical context for 3D brain MRI inpainting while maintaining anatomical consistency [^2609.03956]. Microscopic brain imaging also advances with 3D scattered light imaging, which extracts full 3D fiber orientations from 1D line profiles using an analogical approximation of Rayleigh-Gans scattering theory, validated on brain tissue samples [^2609.03764]. Surface morphometry is unified through tensor-based approaches that combine surface modeling, diffusion smoothing via Laplace-Beltrami estimation, and statistical analysis to localize rapid structural differences in longitudinal pediatric brain images [^2609.03302]. Generative models for structural MRI are being evaluated for clinical utility, with variational autoencoders encoding 3D brain scans into compact latent spaces that capture essential features and exhibit clear clustering patterns for distinguishing Down syndrome from euploid controls [^10.1007_s10044-026-01765-1]. Furthermore, glioma MRI segmentation benchmarks are expanding with the GLI-AL resource, which provides unified eight-class anatomy-lesion labels aligned with multi-modal MRI cases, incorporating previously unlabeled white matter hyperintensities to reduce task-specific label noise [^10.59275_j.melba.2026-575f].

## Brain-Computer Interfaces and Neural Decoding
Proactive assistance systems for cognitive impairment are being enhanced through multimodal sensing. PV-Care integrates wearable EEG with visual environmental perception to detect brain states like learning and memory recall, actively initiating context-aware voice assistance via LLMs, demonstrating high accuracy in real-time cognitive state recognition [^2609.22024]. Hybrid brain-computer interfaces are advancing through low-latency cross-modal generation. Bio-MF introduces a latent-free one-step MeanFlow framework for EEG-conditioned fNIRS generation, achieving an 857x speedup over iterative models while preserving task-relevant hemodynamic structure for real-time motor-imagery decoding [^2609.20904]. Deep learning frameworks are being integrated with signal processing to automatically distinguish resting and cognitive states from EEG records. A CNN-GRU architecture combined with time-frequency analysis achieves high classification accuracy for mathematical, memory, and music tasks, demonstrating the efficacy of automated feature extraction from intricate brain activity [^2609.20467]. General-purpose neural decoding foundation models require robust evaluation protocols. iMINDBench provides a standardized benchmark for intracranial EEG across multiple institutions and naturalistic tasks, revealing that scaling supervised data yields limited gains over within-session training and highlighting the need for improved preprocessing baselines [^2609.18104]. Brain-computer interfaces are being integrated with kinematic data to develop brain-based digital twins for skilled motor imagery. This framework fuses consumer-grade EEG with motion capture to model, predict, and provide real-time feedback on interceptive actions, demonstrating feasibility for rehabilitation and training technologies [^10.17870_bathspa.00017805]. Infant temperament is being correlated with global brain activity patterns measured via EEG microstates, with hypotheses proposing electrophysiological markers for self-regulation and reactivity domains in early development [^10.7273_000007929].

Evaluating the alignment between biological and artificial systems remains a priority, with studies testing two-state access mechanisms in human EEG reproduction and language model simulations to probe global workspace theories [^2609.22300]. Foundation models for brain signals are scaling rapidly; METIS utilizes a unified language-signal alignment framework pretrained on over 70,000 hours of recordings, achieving superior zero-shot and few-shot generalization across diverse datasets without fine-tuning [^2609.15740]. Visual reconstruction from neural activity is advancing through Brain-to-Image generation using GANs, which demonstrates above-chance coarse semantic decoding under repetition-averaged protocols [^2609.22282]. Psychiatric diagnostics are being augmented by transformer frameworks that convert preprocessed EEG into spectrograms for schizophrenia detection, achieving high AUC-ROC scores while preventing subject-related data leakage [^2609.14015]. 3D object reconstruction from brain signals is addressed by Mind2Cloud, a two-granularity diffusion decoding framework that integrates global Transformer and local Point-Voxel CNN branches to adapt to the coarse-to-fine trajectory of denoising [^2609.13991]. Network analysis of EEG data is improved by assumption-lean inference frameworks for spectral differential network analysis, establishing asymptotic normality for de-biased D-trace estimators to study condition-dependent connectivity changes [^2609.13609]. Sample efficiency in neural interfaces is tackled through self-supervised pretraining methods like MAPA, which uses anatomical and relative positional encodings to learn transferable iEEG representations, drastically reducing labeled trial requirements for cross-subject decoding [^2609.13507]. Multimodal decoding strategies like FRIST leverage fMRI-informed spectral projections to guide residual refinement of EEG predictions, significantly improving individual-finger BCI decoding accuracy [^2609.12298]. Additionally, consumer-grade EEG classification for human activities is optimized using Random Forest algorithms, effectively distinguishing real and imagery motor activities despite interpersonal signal variability [^2609.11695]. Clinical neurostimulation is also being refined, with research investigating whether intraoperative therapeutic window data can predict chronic contact selection in deep brain stimulation, aiming to optimize long-term electrode placement [^https:__openalex.org_W7164787306].

Multimodal brain state decoding is enhanced by CoMA-DiT, a bidirectional cross-modal Diffusion Transformer that treats paired modalities as mutual generative supervision sources, achieving substantial accuracy gains in auditory attention and emotion recognition through reliability-gated residual mechanisms [^2609.11341]. In P300 speller design, component anti-synergy challenges the 'more-is-better' paradigm; full-factorial experiments reveal that adding spatial filters or language model priors can degrade performance depending on the underlying EEG pipeline quality, emphasizing conditional component value over additive maximization [^2609.10961]. Cortical information transfer dynamics during high-precision motor tasks like handwriting are mapped using Permutation Transfer Entropy, demonstrating that inter-hemispheric information flow remains functionally equivalent across right- and left-handed individuals when executing tasks with the same anatomical hand, refuting assumptions of mirrored lateralization [^2609.10870]. Federated personalization for motor-imagery BCIs is addressed by NEXUS-MI, a gateway-coordinated framework that treats synchronization as a coupled learning-and-communication control problem, reducing backbone traffic by ~42% while maintaining cohort-level accuracy [^2609.09786]. Source-level analysis of interictal epileptiform discharges is improved by EEGBind, an EEG-centric multimodal binding framework that uses synchronized video-context features as auxiliary evidence to robustly classify five source regions without perturbing the primary EEG representation [^2609.09728].

Security and robustness in neural interfaces are gaining prominence, with the NERVE Attacks class systematically characterizing five orthogonal attack dimensions across the BCI stack, from neural data exfiltration to embedded backdoors, evaluated via the EEGle framework [^2609.08971]. In signal analysis, permutation entropy demonstrates robustness in differentiating closed-eyes and open-eyes resting states from raw EEG, detecting significant differences even with reduced electrode counts and short intervals [^2609.22265]. For real-world applications, EEG-driven decoding frameworks integrate 3D-CRNN models and risk-aware sequential labeling to decode passenger hazard perception in automated vehicles, showing strong generalizability across subjects and sessions [^2609.07128]. Foundation models are scaling to invasive recordings; iBrain jointly pretrains on iEEG and spiking activity using masked signal reconstruction and channel-view alignment, achieving state-of-the-art transferability across diverse settings [^2609.06960]. Clinical neurostimulation is also being optimized through probabilistic sweet spot mapping for deep brain stimulation in essential tremor, comparing Vim and zona incerta targets to refine therapeutic efficacy [^https:__openalex.org_W7205451296].

Implantable neural interfaces are pushing the boundaries of wireless communication and decoding fidelity. Bidirectional wireless links for weakly coupled implantable BCIs are being benchmarked across multiple modalities, highlighting the need for sub-10 pJ/b wideband techniques like ultra-wideband to support million-channel interfaces within strict tissue-heating limits [^2609.05786]. Speech restoration from neural activity is advancing through Brain2Speech-Net, a single-stage framework that bypasses intermediate text decoding by using a differentiable phoneme bottleneck and a lightweight deep-HMM aligner, achieving real-time intelligibility under limited data constraints [^2609.04455]. Cross-subject generalization in non-invasive decoding is addressed by LibriBrain100, an extended MEG dataset supporting the 2026 PNPL competition's Broad track, which progressively reduces subject-specific fine-tuning data to clinically feasible durations for word classification [^2609.03231]. Large-scale intracranial recording datasets are also being standardized; the Brain Treebank provides processed BIDS-iEEG format recordings from naturalistic movie viewing, enabling robust analysis of neural responses to complex linguistic and visual stimuli [^10.82901_nemar.nm000253]. Novel sensing modalities are emerging, with thermal imagery-based BCIs proposed to capture physiological responses for non-invasive brain-computer interaction, expanding the toolkit beyond traditional electrical and optical methods [^10.3217_978-3-99161-093-9-036].

## Neurological Disorders and Brain Cancer Therapeutics
Brain disorder classification from neuroimaging data is advanced by adaptive multi-scale spatial hypergraph networks that integrate hypergraph attention and spatial-channel mechanisms to model higher-order dependencies, achieving state-of-the-art performance across autism, depression, and Alzheimer's benchmark datasets [^2609.19755]. Therapeutic strategies for Alzheimer's disease are being critically re-evaluated regarding amyloid precursor protein (APP) suppression. Analyses argue that APP functions as a universal pro-survival molecule deployed during catastrophic neural insults, suggesting that broad-spectrum suppression via RNA interference risks disarming the brain's intrinsic repair mechanisms [^10.5281_zenodo.20049820]. Liquid biopsy approaches for glioblastoma diagnostics are advancing through cell-free DNA analysis. Research on nucleosome positioning in paired tumor and normal tissues highlights specific transcription factor binding sites as potential biomarkers, offering non-invasive alternatives to invasive tissue biopsies for early detection [^10.5526_err-00042862]. Genomic repetitive elements in cell-free DNA are being leveraged for pan-cancer and tissue-specific diagnostics. Machine learning models trained on nucleosome signatures at DNA repeats successfully discriminate healthy individuals from glioblastoma patients, establishing a foundation for repeat-based cfDNA profiling in early cancer detection [^10.5526_err-00042900]. Spatially resolved single-cell atlases are being constructed to map pan-cancer metastatic brain tumors, with new data releases providing 10x Visium datasets for analysis [^10.5281_zenodo.21816284]. Targeted gene silencing strategies for glioblastoma are being enhanced through lipid nanoparticle delivery systems. LNP-encapsulated siRNA targeting TERT demonstrates robust knockdown of oncogenic transcripts, significantly reducing cell viability and offering a promising approach to overcome blood-brain barrier penetration challenges in brain cancer therapy [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059253]. Molecular signaling pathways driving breast cancer metastasis to the brain are being investigated, with code repositories released for studies on RET signaling as a mediator in estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer brain metastasis [^10.5281_zenodo.19501083]. Extracellular vesicles derived from neural crest stem cells are being explored as therapeutic agents for glioblastoma, inhibiting tumor proliferation and migration by modulating NF-κB and IRF3 pathways [^10.48683_1926.00128572]. Novel lipid-based nanoparticle formulations are being developed to enhance drug delivery across the blood-brain barrier for glioblastoma treatment, showing promise for targeted brain tumor therapy [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059033]. Mitochondrial quality control in Parkinson's disease is being linked to aerobic exercise interventions, proposing a context-dependent framework where exercise reshapes nitric oxide signaling toward adaptive profiles that influence mitochondrial biogenesis and mitophagy initiation [^10.4103_mgr.medgasres-d-26-00008].

Disease progression modeling is being inverted to address incubation periods in progressive disorders; reverse spatio-temporal modeling using Neural ODEs successfully reconstructs unobserved healthier brain anatomy from later diseased states in Alzheimer's cohorts [^2609.14590]. Cognitive aging research highlights resting-state EEG markers, particularly alpha oscillations and aperiodic activity, as sensitive biomarkers for sustained attention and memory decline, offering pathways to detect subtle neural plasticity from lifestyle interventions [^10.25907_00976]. The microbiota-gut-brain axis in Parkinson's disease is further elucidated through preclinical models demonstrating bidirectional pathology transmission, with TLR4 inhibition showing therapeutic potential for motor and GI symptoms [^https:__openalex.org_W7213566724]. Genetic epilepsy syndromes are being characterized through case studies of bilateral open-lip schizencephaly complicated by West syndrome, identifying de novo PAFAH1B1 frameshift variants as pathogenic and emphasizing the need for early genetic evaluation [^10.3760_cma.j.cn511374-20260302-00092]. Nanotechnology advances glioblastoma treatment through PEGylated chitosan-gold nanoparticles engineered for radiosensitization and targeted siRNA delivery, enhancing therapeutic efficacy across the blood-brain barrier [^https:__openalex.org_W7204677537]. Neuroinflammation in Alzheimer's disease is mitigated by engineered mesenchymal stem cell-derived extracellular vesicles overexpressing miR-146a, which suppress the NR4A3–NF-κB axis to reduce amyloid-β, tau phosphorylation, and cognitive decline in murine models [^10.4103_nrr.nrr-d-25-00404]. Finally, large-scale visualization techniques for α-synuclein oligomers in Parkinson's disease brain tissue provide critical insights into pathological aggregation patterns, supporting the development of targeted diagnostic and therapeutic strategies [^10.5281_zenodo.18548364].

Foundation models for fMRI are being organized by measured learning relations rather than flat sampling. BrainTaskonomy employs a proxy model to estimate difficulty and directed facilitation across domains, yielding a priority-guided curriculum that reduces prediction errors and reveals asymmetric, target-dependent transfer across fifteen downstream tasks [^2609.10518]. Graph neural networks operating on covariance matrices (VNNs) offer theoretical advantages over PCA-based pipelines for characterizing brain age gaps in neurodegeneration, providing refined stability bounds against finite-sample perturbations and demonstrating transferability across multiscale datasets [^2609.10490]. Explainable AI loops for brain-age prediction, such as XAI-Refine, automate the conversion of post-hoc explanations into neurobiological questions, retrieving verified literature to impose differentiable constraints that align model mechanisms with established knowledge while preserving predictive performance [^2609.09388]. Blood-brain barrier permeability prediction is streamlined through an explainable ML framework using RDKit molecular descriptors, where optimized XGBoost classifiers identify TPSA, HBD, and LogP as key determinants, offering efficient early-stage screening for CNS therapeutics [^2609.10012]. In Alzheimer's research, differential DNA methylation in blood is identified as a potential mediator linking ambient PM2.5 exposure to cerebrospinal fluid biomarkers, highlighting environmental epigenetic impacts on pre-clinical AD stages [^10.1038_s41380-026-03662-9]. Schizophrenia therapeutics are advancing with muscarinic agonists like xanomeline (KarXT), which target M1-M5 receptors to restore disrupted glutamatergic and dopaminergic circuits, offering trans-diagnostic benefits for cognitive and negative symptoms with reduced metabolic side effects [^10.1111_bph.70582]. Brain metastases from colorectal cancer demonstrate higher local failure rates after stereotactic radiosurgery compared to other primaries, underscoring the radioresistant biology of gastrointestinal histologies [^10.1093_nop_npag050]. Thalamic metastases are effectively managed with SRS, showing high cumulative local control rates and supporting its role as a safe, non-invasive treatment option [^10.1227_neuprac.0000000000000284].

AI-driven segmentation continues to evolve for clinical oncology. Cancer segmentation models are being audited via internal concepts, where sparse autoencoders decompose activations to detect a "Feeling of Error" signature, outperforming output-based uncertainty methods in prostate, pancreatic, and brain cancer segmentation [^2609.08879]. For glioblastoma and other brain tumors, frameworks like LASSNet condition spatial-semantic fusion on modality availability and feature hierarchy, achieving robust Dice scores across 15 non-empty modality configurations without reconstructing missing inputs [^2609.06733]. Similarly, Uni-Light introduces an ultra-lightweight architecture with uncertainty-aware knowledge distillation, reducing parameters by 97.56% while surpassing SOTA Dice scores, demonstrating that teacher uncertainty can prioritize hard-to-classify regions [^2609.06729]. In traumatic brain injury, multiverse analyses of clustering algorithms reveal substantial instability across distance metrics and algorithm choices, underscoring that clustering solutions should not be blindly interpreted as underlying structure for prognostic modeling [^2609.08577]. Epilepsy research benefits from released structural connectome datasets for temporal lobe epilepsy, enabling graph signal processing analyses of structure-function coupling and the Structural Decoupling Index [^10.5281_zenodo.20845920].

Understanding the molecular and circuit-level underpinnings of neurological disorders is accelerating through multi-omic atlases and targeted interventions. The BICAN_BG_Paired-Tag project releases a comprehensive single-nucleus multi-omic atlas of the adult human basal ganglia, jointly measuring histone modifications and transcriptomes to delineate cell-type-resolved regulatory programs and identify conserved neuronal circuits alongside disease-associated divergent programs [^10.5281_zenodo.18458423]. Environmental neurotoxicity is being linked to behavioral phenotypes through cohort studies showing that polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon exposure covaries with gut microbiome composition and neurobehavioral traits like impulsivity and attention, establishing a three-way relationship mediated by microbial biotransformation [^10.1128_spectrum.00783-26]. Non-invasive neuromodulation is being refined for disorders of consciousness, with focused ultrasound of the central lateral thalamus tested in non-human primates, revealing that distributed network stimulation may warrant investigation as an alternative to focal targeting for arousal modulation [^10.64898_2026.09.15.751608]. Dementia research continues to integrate multi-level analyses, from molecular mechanisms like protein misfolding and circulating biomarkers to population-level determinants, emphasizing that cognitive decline can only be fully understood by combining motor, cardiovascular, immune, and genetic regulatory pathways [^https:__openalex.org_W7204678580].

## Neural Mechanisms, Connectomics, and Signaling
Artificial neural networks are being re-evaluated through biological lenses, with proposals to incorporate neuromodulation principles. Position papers argue that modeling neurotransmitter signaling as a third axis of learning, alongside activity and plasticity, could significantly enhance the adaptability and long-term stability of artificial systems [^2609.20083]. Bayesian neural networks are being utilized to study how the brain constructs accurate internal models from limited experience. Research indicates that moderate neural noise is critical for faithful internal simulation of rare events, restoring marginal and conditional occurrence frequencies that deterministic replay fails to capture [^2609.18033]. Complex network analysis of resting-state functional connectomes is advanced through graphlet fingerprints that capture local-to-mesotopology differences with high sensitivity, highlighting spatially localized connectivity alterations in schizophrenia [^2609.17445]. Transcranial ultrasound stimulation safety and efficacy are improved through optimized acoustic lens design, with research on phase unwrapping for bifocal lenses demonstrating significant improvements in spatial precision and reduction of out-of-target energy deposition across human skull models [^2609.18495]. Cognitive performance in category fluency tasks is linked to structural brain integrity and cultural factors, revealing age-related differences in gray matter volumes in prefrontal and temporal regions that influence cognitive strategies across different cultural backgrounds [^10.48617_etd.1671]. The organization of sensory maps in the brain is being clarified through spatial coding mechanisms, revealing that olfactory receptor choice follows a coherent dorsoventral transcriptional code that aligns receptor maps in the nose with precise topographical organization in the brain [^10.5281_zenodo.18941307]. Gut-brain signaling mechanisms are being elucidated through vagal afferent pathways, demonstrating that local CCK release modulates satiety and food intake, with high-fat diets blunting central CCK signaling and linking peripheral metabolism to central neural regulation [^10.7273_000008411]. Inter-organ metabolite exchange is being quantified through arteriovenous profiling across human organs, revealing significant brain-specific uptake and release patterns that provide a quantitative foundation for understanding cross-organ metabolic interactions [^10.5281_zenodo.20652506].

Biologically-inspired computing is advancing through scaled hippocampus-inspired spiking neural networks demonstrated on neuromorphic memristive hardware, utilizing a network downscaling methodology to preserve biologically-realistic resting-state dynamics with high energy efficiency [^2609.16429]. Evolutionary neuroscience challenges strict cognitive buffer hypotheses by showing that larger neural networks in dynamic environments primarily arise from prior static evolution, highlighting the role of evolutionary history in brain size adaptation [^2609.15569]. Digital twin fidelity is improved by a new stochastic model combining Random Neural Networks and Markov-Modulated Poisson Processes, mapped to Discrete Event System Specifications for executable whole-brain simulation [^2609.13973]. Functional connectivity analysis benefits from a novel nonparametric correlation estimator based on cubic equations, enabling time-varying correlation estimation and correcting boundary effects in dynamic brain network studies [^2609.13848]. Foundation models for brain dynamics are evaluated through perturbational validity frameworks, demonstrating that input-driven pretraining yields lower errors in recovering controlled flow and landscape geometry compared to passive prediction models [^2609.12710]. Cortical mapping of sensory stimuli is refined using Bayesian semiparametric hidden Markov random partition fields, which promote sparsity in factor influence partitions and capture subtle local variations across the brain's cortical graph [^2609.13319].

Neuronal avalanche analysis is standardized by pyAvalanches, an open-source Python package that computes Avalanche Transition Matrices to map spatiotemporal propagation patterns in EEG recordings, bridging network topology with dynamical interactions and enabling reproducible biomarker development [^2609.11530]. The 'Platonic brain bridge' hypothesis proposes that omni-modal LLMs converge on brain-like representations usable bidirectionally; aligning frozen model experts to human cortical networks significantly boosts benchmark accuracy, while sparse feature localization confirms human brain organization as a viable architectural prior [^2609.10947]. Scalable neuronal reconstruction from petabyte-scale microscopy is achieved via SkNeXt, a topology-first framework that converts morphology into compact SWC skeletons, using them as spatial indices to selectively retrieve high-resolution data and drastically reduce I/O overhead [^2609.09832]. Criticality in neural activity models emerges naturally without fine-tuning as system size increases; inverse problem inference concentrates parameters near critical points, with mouse brain recordings collapsing to first-order phase transitions in Ising models [^2609.09438]. Finally, astrocytic Connexin 43 gap junctional deficits are causally linked to alpha-synuclein aggregation and inflammation in Parkinson's disease models, where pharmacological preservation of gap junctions reduces pathology, suggesting Cx43 as a novel therapeutic target [^https:__openalex.org_W7211218910]. Additionally, PAR2 activation reduces astrocytic reactivity and amyloid plaque load in female 5xFAD mice, indicating that targeting glial reactivity via protease-activated receptors may mitigate neuroinflammation in amyloid pathology [^https:__openalex.org_W7160740749].

Advances in connectomics and cellular imaging are expanding our understanding of neural architecture. Hi-M workflows adapted for Drosophila brain cryosections enable chromatin tracing while preserving tissue architecture, providing robust approaches for studying 3D genome organization in intact tissues [^2609.08776]. In neurodynamics, phase oscillator networks with state-dependent delays capture white matter plasticity effects, suggesting that myelin changes driven by neuronal activity can drive networks toward more coherent behavior [^2609.08619]. Learning mechanisms in neural microcircuits are being reformulated as state separation problems, enabling online feedback learning via gradient tunneling that derives credit assignment from local synaptic spike timing [^2609.08070]. Criticality in neural systems is being probed model-agnostically using the Fisher Information Metric, which reliably tracks proximity to criticality across branching processes to whole-brain models without requiring true control parameters [^2609.07624]. Theoretical frameworks are also exploring nonlocal entangled nerve fibers, with empirical analyses of collective behaviors supporting the Liu-Chen-Ao hypothesis and highlighting the necessity of adaptive entangled game modules in AGI architectures [^2609.09226]. At the circuit level, rodent virtual-reality assays and genetic mapping are dissecting neuronal pathways in the retrosplenial cortex, characterizing sensory and spatial inputs that update internal spatial maps during navigation [^https:__openalex.org_W7212408405]. Finally, large-scale behavioral and imaging studies reveal a cerebellar motor reserve, showing that predictive motor control remains remarkably maintained into advanced old age despite widespread neural degeneration, dissociating preserved feedforward mechanisms from vulnerable feedback-based control [^https:__openalex.org_W7213844090].

Brain-inspired computing and neural dynamics are being explored through novel architectures and theoretical frameworks. Physical neuromorphic nanowire networks demonstrate fully autonomous closed-loop prediction of chaotic time series, with virtual node and non-virtual node approaches reproducing qualitative attractor structures over long horizons [^2609.06395]. Brain-inspired neural processing units utilizing spiking neural networks are being applied to wireless positioning, translating channel state information into user positions with high energy efficiency compared to conventional machine learning models [^2609.06252]. Connectome-to-function mapping is advanced by conditional generative latent frameworks that encode high-dimensional connectome graphs into compact structural spaces, enabling reconstruction and generation of candidate connectomes while capturing functional variation in reservoir computing experiments [^2609.06093]. Hierarchical zero-shot frameworks functionally inspired by human brain division of roles are being deployed for robot task reasoning, grounding language commands in explicit object states and verifying physical outcomes through closed-loop execution [^2609.05985]. Functional brain network analysis benefits from Bayesian frameworks that recover shared latent community structures from multiple noisy network realizations and auxiliary covariates, improving recovery when network signals are weak [^2609.05654]. Robust stabilization of control systems is being achieved through spiking neuronal communication, where integrate-and-fire spike encoders and synaptic-inspired decoders enable practical input-to-state stability for nonlinear and linear time-invariant plants [^2609.05054]. Neuronal voltage dynamics are efficiently modeled using parameter-conditioned Fourier Neural Operators as surrogates for the FitzHugh-Nagumo system, capturing both oscillatory and excitable regimes with sub-0.1% relative error and orders-of-magnitude speedups [^2609.04549]. Magnetoencephalography foundation models are being positioned through a comprehensive roadmap covering tokenization, backbone architectures, and multi-modal integration, addressing the field's need for reusable models pretrained on broad neural datasets [^2609.04461].
