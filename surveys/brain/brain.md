# Living Survey: brain

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **brain**.

## Brain Tumor Diagnostics and Segmentation

Recent advances in medical imaging and AI have significantly improved the detection and segmentation of brain tumors. NeuroTS-Net introduces a three-dimensional encoder-decoder architecture that preserves fine intensity and boundary information for pediatric brain tumor segmentation, outperforming baseline methods on the BraTS 2026 dataset [^2609.16873]. Similarly, DE-GAN employs a contrast-enhancing conditional GAN to synthesize slice-adaptive FLAIR images, improving segmentation of enhancing tumors and tumor cores across multiple BraTS datasets [^2609.16755]. For comprehensive diagnosis, BrainVLM integrates MRI scans, demographics, and radiology reports to classify all 12 WHO 2021 brain tumor types, providing diagnostic uncertainty quantification and report generation [^2609.16597]. The generalization of conventional models like nnU-Net across heterogeneous populations has also been evaluated, revealing challenges with small or disconnected tumor components [^2609.15524]. Furthermore, machine unlearning techniques like GRIN+ address privacy regulations in imbalanced medical data, maintaining diagnostic accuracy while erasing sensitive patient records [^2609.15571].

Beyond imaging, liquid biopsy and epigenetic profiling offer non-invasive diagnostic avenues. Analyses of cell-free DNA (cfDNA) reveal nucleosome positioning signatures in glioblastoma multiforme (GBM), providing potential biomarkers for early detection [^10.5526_err-00042862][^10.5526_err-00042900]. Single-cell spatially resolved atlases of pan-cancer metastatic brain tumors are being developed to map the tumor microenvironment at unprecedented resolution [^10.5281_zenodo.21782884][^10.5281_zenodo.21782885][^10.5281_zenodo.21816284][^10.5281_zenodo.21816285]. Additionally, secretomes and extracellular vesicles from neural crest-derived stem cells have demonstrated the ability to inhibit GBM proliferation and modulate immune responses [^10.48683_1926.00128572].

## Neural Decoding and EEG Analysis

Electroencephalography (EEG) and brain signal analysis continue to benefit from large-scale benchmarks and foundation models. iMINDBench establishes a standardized framework for iEEG neural decoding across multiple institutions and naturalistic tasks, highlighting the need for models that generalize across subjects and preprocessing pipelines [^2609.18104]. For broader brain signal analysis, METIS introduces a language-guided multimodal foundation model pretrained on over 70,000 hours of recordings, achieving state-of-the-art zero-shot and few-shot performance across 12 datasets without fine-tuning [^2609.15740]. In psychiatric applications, CNN-Transformer hybrids for schizophrenia detection from EEG spectrograms achieve high classification accuracy while ensuring subject-level data partitioning to prevent leakage [^2609.14015]. 

Advancements in generative decoding also show promise, with Mind2Cloud proposing a two-granularity diffusion framework to reconstruct 3D point clouds from EEG signals, dynamically adapting spatial granularity to match the coarse-to-fine denoising trajectory [^2609.13991]. Furthermore, nonparametric correlation estimators are being applied to analyze dynamic functional connectivity across six frequency bands, offering new insights into neural interactions [^2609.13848]. In developmental neuroscience, EEG microstates are being linked to infant temperament attributes, providing a window into early self-regulation and emotional reactivity [^10.7273_000007929].

## Computational Neuroscience and Digital Twins

Theoretical and computational models are increasingly bridging biological realism with hardware efficiency. Scaled hippocampus-inspired spiking neural networks are being mapped to neuromorphic memristive hardware, demonstrating biologically realistic resting-state dynamics and energy efficiency [^2609.16429]. At the systems level, brain-based digital twins (BB-DTs) are being formalized to integrate EEG with kinematic data for real-time motor imagery feedback, validated through synthetic data augmentation and closed-loop coaching interfaces [^10.17870_bathspa.00017805]. A new stochastic model combining Random Neural Networks and Markov-Modulated Poisson Processes aims to improve the fidelity of whole-brain digital twins by capturing mechanisms across multiple scales [^2609.13973]. 

Neuroevolutionary studies also challenge traditional hypotheses, showing that larger neural networks in dynamic environments often arise from prior static evolution rather than direct adaptation to changing conditions [^2609.15569]. On the molecular level, circular RNAs (circRNAs) are identified as stable biomarkers of biological aging in the brain, accumulating at rates corresponding to physiological rather than chronological age [^10.48617_etd.1568]. Additionally, moderate neural noise has been shown to be critical for accurate internal simulation of rare events in Bayesian neural networks, compensating for sampling errors and offering a framework for understanding disorders like Parkinson's [^2609.18033]. Graphlet-based structural fingerprints are also proving effective for capturing local-to-mesoscopic topology in complex networks, including resting-state functional connectomes [^2609.17445].

## Neurodegeneration, Metabolism, and Sensory Systems

Longitudinal disease progression modeling is being revolutionized by reverse spatio-temporal approaches. By using Neural Ordinary Differential Equations in a discrete latent space, models can now reconstruct unobserved healthier brain anatomy from later diseased states, aiding early intervention strategies for Alzheimer's disease [^2609.14590]. The amyloid precursor protein (APP) is also being re-evaluated not merely as a pathological substrate but as a universal survival molecule, raising critical questions about broad-spectrum APP suppression therapies [^10.5281_zenodo.20049820]. 

Metabolic profiling of human arteriovenous blood samples has revealed extensive cross-organ metabolite exchange, identifying 245 significant instances of organ-specific uptake and release, including brain-specific patterns [^10.5281_zenodo.20652505][^10.5281_zenodo.20652506]. In Parkinson's disease, aerobic exercise is proposed to reshape nitric oxide signaling toward adaptive profiles that may enhance mitophagy and neuroprotection [^10.4103_mgr.medgasres-d-26-00008]. 

Sensory and developmental neurobiology continues to uncover intricate mapping mechanisms. A spatial code governing olfactory receptor choice aligns sensory maps in both the nose and brain through a dorsoventral gradient in retinoic acid signaling [^10.5281_zenodo.18941307][^10.5281_zenodo.18941308]. In the hindbrain, cholecystokinin (CCK) signaling in the nucleus of the solitary tract is blunted by high-fat diets, linking central vagal afferents to satiety and obesity pathways [^10.7273_000008411]. Finally, gray matter volumes in semantic control regions are shown to interact with age and culture, influencing cognitive categorization and retrieval strategies [^10.48617_etd.1671].
