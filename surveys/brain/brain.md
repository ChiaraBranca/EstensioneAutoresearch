# Living Survey: brain

## Introduzione

Questo documento raccoglie la letteratura scientifica sul tema **brain**.

## Advanced MRI & Image Processing

Recent advancements in medical imaging have significantly improved the resolution, speed, and interpretability of brain scans. Deep learning models are increasingly integrating physical priors to enhance segmentation. M-Net introduces matrix spectral analysis and vector calculus operators into U-Net, demonstrating that mathematical inductive biases provide effective complementary information for medical image segmentation [^2608.12196]. Similarly, GenFAR leverages a modular deep learning framework trained on nearly 50,000 MRIs to learn general, clinically informed features, improving sample efficiency for downstream tasks [^2608.12185]. In the context of Alzheimer's disease, benchmarking of CNN architectures on single-view MRI scans highlights the difficulty in classifying early-stage dementia, with VGG16 achieving the highest accuracy among tested models [^2608.11762].

Foundation models for brain MRI are evolving to be more robust and expandable. A continually expandable foundation model for brain MRI uses pruning techniques to allow sequential expansion across clinical domains without forgetting earlier capabilities [^2608.08319]. However, audits reveal that frozen foundation model embeddings can act as "site fingerprints," encoding acquisition site statistics rather than just anatomy, which poses challenges for federated learning [^2608.10295]. To address data efficiency, MRIComp4Flow demonstrates that lossy compression (JPEG2000) can be used to train 3D generative models with synthesis quality statistically equivalent to uncompressed data within a pre-specified margin [^2608.10291].

Image registration and super-resolution techniques are also advancing. A Joint Affine-Diffeomorphic framework estimates global and local motions simultaneously, outperforming sequential baselines in brain MRI registration [^2608.10769]. For motion artifacts, self-supervised representation learning is employed to reduce distortions in 3D brain MRI without paired training data [^2608.10170]. Super-resolution is addressed by a diffusion bridge model that reconstructs high-resolution MRI from low-resolution inputs in just ten sampling steps, preserving fine structures and lesions [^2608.08819]. Additionally, cross-contrast super-resolution of anisotropic T2w MRI is enabled by methods that improve downstream segmentation by enforcing anatomical consistency across views [^2608.08401].

Quantitative mapping and fetal imaging are also benefiting from these methods. CUPA-T2* propagates reconstruction uncertainty to downstream T2* fitting, improving accuracy in accelerated MRI [^2608.08693]. DisMorph disentangles longitudinal deformation into technical and anatomical transforms, reducing bias from scanner distortions in Alzheimer's disease studies [^2608.08173]. A vendor-agnostic method for joint T1, T2, and myelin water fraction mapping with motion correction ensures consistency across different scanners [^2608.07687]. For fetal development, a deep learning framework provides reliable and reproducible brain biometry from 3D super-resolution-reconstructed fetal MRI [^2608.03724]. Furthermore, the diagnostic robustness of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) under perturbations in brain MRI datasets reveals significant vulnerabilities in presentation-order stability, highlighting the need for stability-based metrics in clinical deployment [^2608.04885].

## EEG, BCI & Neural Decoding

Electroencephalography (EEG) and Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI) are seeing new paradigms in representation learning and decoding. Continuous-latent predictive modeling reformulates EEG decoding as a continuous semantic embedding prediction problem, aligning continuous neural dynamics with natural language semantics [^2608.11656]. In neural tracking, a framework using null-normalized tracking scores provides a more interpretable measure of stimulus-response relationships than raw correlations [^2608.10887].

Functional connectivity analysis is enhanced by a federated learning framework that decomposes site effects to model dynamic functional connectivity (dFC) in multi-site fMRI data, improving diagnosis of ASD and ADHD [^2608.07393]. For pediatric headaches, a foundation model approach using rs-fMRI data outperforms standard functional connectivity matrices in classification tasks [^2608.07287].

BCI systems are becoming more robust to domain shifts. An online source-free UDA framework corrects geometric bias on the Riemannian manifold caused by class imbalance in EEG data [^2608.05315]. NeuroPB scales neural decoding by transferring knowledge from pretrained behavioral representations, improving trajectory decoding in macaque datasets with limited neural data [^2608.04389].

Theoretical and computational aspects of brain activity are also explored. SheafIQ quantifies vector field organization on geometric graphs, revealing complementary information in functional brain networks [^2608.08728]. The "LaFountaine Brain Paradox" framework examines how finite biological substrates support unbounded internal representational dynamics [^10.5281_zenodo.17665509]. In social contexts, EEG and fNIRS studies show that social competition and secrecy burden share neural mechanisms involving the prefrontal cortex and amygdala, leading to cognitive exhaustion [^10.5281_zenodo.19075671]. Sustained attention and memory in aging are linked to resting-state alpha oscillations and aperiodic activity, offering sensitive biomarkers for cognitive decline [^10.25907_00976]. Infant temperament is correlated with EEG microstates, providing insights into early self-regulation [^10.7273_000007929].

## Neurodegeneration, Pathology & Therapeutics

Research into brain diseases and therapeutics is expanding. Mean-field models bridge molecular changes to large-scale brain activity, illustrating how synaptic receptor changes can impact global brain function, such as in anesthesia [^2608.11185]. Neural implants safety is addressed by single-fault detection mechanisms in DC-coupled recording front ends to prevent tissue damage [^2608.10361]. Repeated head impacts cause damage-mediated amplification of brain deformation, increasing injury risk estimates beyond what damage-free models predict [^2608.10331].

Metabolic and energy aspects of brain function are critical. Reduced Gibbs free energy supply from ATP hydrolysis impairs protein function and neuronal information processing during mental fatigue [^2608.10211]. The gut-brain axis is implicated in neuroinflammation; *Saccharomyces boulardii* mitigates LPS-induced neuroinflammation in the hippocampus by modulating the TLR4/NF-κB pathway [^10.1016_j.ibneur.2026.06.008]. Similarly, *Clostridioides difficile* colitis promotes neuroinflammatory responses in the prefrontal cortex [^10.5281_zenodo.20086102]. A liver-brain feedback loop driven by the PVN-DMV-Liver circuit accelerates hepatocellular carcinoma progression in the context of depression comorbidity [^10.5281_zenodo.17662296].

Brain tumors, particularly Glioblastoma (GBM), remain a major challenge. Text-guided refinement using vision-language models improves glioma subregion segmentation [^2608.05389]. NeuroMosaic, a 3D multimodal language model, aligns MRI tokens with clinical narratives for grounded glioma reasoning [^2608.03187]. Liquid biopsies using cell-free DNA (cfDNA) nucleosome signatures show promise for GBM diagnostics [^10.5526_err-00042862]. Lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) and nanostructured lipid carriers (NLCs) loaded with anticancer compounds (like EGCG or siRNA targeting TERT) are being developed to cross the blood-brain barrier and treat GBM [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059253, ^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059033]. Secretomes from neural crest-derived stem cells inhibit GBM proliferation and modulate immune responses [^10.48683_1926.00128572].

Alzheimer's Disease (AD) research continues to evolve. The Amyloid Precursor Protein (APP) is argued to be a pro-survival molecule, and its suppression via RNAi may be harmful [^10.5281_zenodo.20049820]. Breast cancer brain metastases (BCBM) exhibit distinct immune landscapes, with spatial transcriptomics revealing heterogeneity in the immune niche [^10.5281_zenodo.17723784]. Deep brain stimulation (STN-DBS) for Parkinson's disease is associated with long-term outcomes, though older age at implantation increases mortality risk [^2608.05609].

## Computational Neuroscience & SNNs

Biologically plausible computing models are gaining traction. SuperNeuroMAT is a fast, matrix-based simulator for Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs), supporting dense and sparse execution modes efficiently [^2608.08479]. Learning rules that respect Dale's constraint (excitatory/inhibitory neurons) are proposed, using non-negative channels to represent positive and negative contributions, enabling backpropagation-like learning with local interactions [^2608.06963]. NeuMoSync integrates dynamic, neuron-specific modulation into deep networks to enhance plasticity in continual learning, inspired by global neuromodulatory mechanisms [^2608.04358].

Predictive coding principles are explored through a recurrent algorithm variant, showing that hierarchical predictions can emerge from local contrastive learning [^2608.05481]. A biologically plausible pruning rule for recurrent networks preserves task performance by sampling connections based on noisy fluctuations [^2608.05464].

Brain activity dynamics are further characterized by non-linear methods. A study on conserved multimodal nonlinear dynamics finds that EEG frequency bands follow a power law and that consciousness stability depends on a bounded relationship between temporal transport and geometric support [^10.5281_zenodo.20138765]. Persistent homology in structural connectomes reveals that topology-informed approaches broaden the controllable subspace in human structural connectomes [^2608.03181].

## Therapeutics & Drug Delivery

Novel drug delivery systems are being engineered to target the brain. Microbubbles combined with ultrasound are shown to be effective for gene therapy, cancer treatment, and bridging the blood-brain barrier [^10.5281_zenodo.19676282]. Geometry-informed parameter-efficient fine-tuning of molecular GNNs is used to predict blood-brain barrier permeability, aiding CNS drug discovery [^2608.04257].

## Theoretical & Interdisciplinary Perspectives

Interdisciplinary approaches link brain function to broader systems. A comprehensive human arteriovenous metabolic profiling atlas reveals cross-organ metabolite exchange, including brain-specific uptake and release [^10.5281_zenodo.20652505]. Olfactory receptor choice is governed by a spatial code that aligns sensory maps in the nose and brain [^10.5281_zenodo.18941307]. In Drosophila, circRNAs accumulate in the brain with age, serving as biomarkers of biological aging [^10.48617_etd.1568]. RET signaling is identified as a mediator in estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer brain metastasis [^10.5281_zenodo.19501083].

## Brain Metastasis & Neuro-Oncology

The intersection of oncology and neurology is increasingly explored through advanced imaging and molecular profiling. RET signaling has been identified as a key mediator in the brain metastasis of estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer, highlighting specific molecular pathways that facilitate tumor colonization in the brain [^10.5281_zenodo.19501083]. In the realm of pediatric neuro-oncology, neuroblastoma (a tumor of the sympathetic nervous system often involving the brain/spine) is being studied via circulating tumor cells (CTCs). Single-cell mass spectrometry of CTCs from patients with neuroblastoma, alongside other solid tumors, provides a detailed molecular profile of metastatic cells, offering potential biomarkers for early detection and monitoring [^10.5281_zenodo.20779109, ^10.5281_zenodo.20779108].

## Brain-Computer Interfaces & Digital Twins

Digital twins and interactive systems are being developed for brain applications. A brain-based digital twin (BB-DT) fuses EEG with kinematic data to model skilled interceptive movements, providing real-time feedback for motor imagery training [^10.17870_bathspa.00017805]. RAG-Audio improves brain-to-audio reconstruction by retrieving real-audio exemplars to initialize generative models [^2608.09331]. Mesh evolution models (MT-GNN) predict brain morphometry changes in continuous time using graph-based metric tensor embeddings [^2608.05132].
