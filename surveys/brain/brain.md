# Living Survey: brain

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **brain**.

## Neural Decoding and Brain-Computer Interfaces

Recent advances in brain-to-language decoding have expanded the field from constrained recognition to text generation and streaming personalized speech, synthesizing developments across invasive and non-invasive measurements while highlighting the need for shared benchmarks and longitudinal studies [^2609.27650]. To address session variability in invasive brain-machine interfaces, Task-Conditioned Latent Alignment (TCLA) stabilizes neural decoding by learning a shared latent space aligned separately for each task condition, significantly reducing failure rates across cross-subject and long-term evaluations [^2609.27441]. In the realm of signal preprocessing, attention-guided conditional adversarial learning (P2P Trans) effectively suppresses electromyographic artifacts in single-channel EEG, demonstrating superior spectral restoration under severe contamination [^2609.26772]. Furthermore, modeling long-horizon EEG sequences benefits from neuroscience-inspired tokenization; Brain-Token Learning replaces fixed temporal windows with microstate-derived brain tokens, capturing quasi-stable large-scale brain states and improving representation learning across affective and clinical datasets [^2609.24324]. Optimizing cross-subject EEG decoding remains challenging due to gradient nonstationarity, but adaptive forgetting controllers like AFOR dynamically adjust second-moment decay coefficients, yielding robust performance improvements over fixed optimizers [^2609.24233]. Developmental neuroscience also explores these techniques, with proposed frameworks linking infant temperament attributes to wakeful resting-state EEG microstates to investigate early self-regulation and emotional reactivity [^10.7273_000007929]. Brain-to-image retrieval systems are also evolving rapidly, with NeuroGlyph learning a trial-independent visual target across multiple depths of a frozen visual backbone to outperform final-layer supervision on THINGS-EEG and THINGS-MEG datasets [^2609.24136]. To address spatial mixing in scalp EEG, adaptive cortically constrained alignment methods reconstruct responses into ROI-level source patterns, achieving strong zero-shot retrieval performance while providing post hoc interpretability [^2609.24109]. For language decoding, Hierarchical Dynamic Neural Decoding (HDND) treats word retrieval as structured refinement, combining intermediate neural representations and contextual semantic predictions to yield higher balanced Top-10 estimates across seven EEG and MEG datasets in multiple languages [^2609.24095]. Auditing EEG foundation models on motor imagery reveals that foundation models and task-specific decoders evaluated with matched input pipelines show architecture-dependent accuracy differences, highlighting the need for standardized evaluation protocols [^2609.23924]. In clinical monitoring, chaotic dynamics-regulated topological learning partitions EEG signals into discrete functional subnets and models nodes as Lorenz oscillators to reliably distinguish preictal from interictal states in patient-specific networks [^2609.23317]. Large-scale neural recording benchmarks like BrainWideBench demonstrate that pretraining on multi-region recordings from hundreds of mice improves cross-animal transfer for decoding behavior and predicting masked neural activity, though gains remain highly dependent on objective alignment [^2609.22064]. Furthermore, proactive AI assistance systems like PV-Care integrate wearable EEG with visual environmental perception to detect cognitive states (Learning, Memory Recall, Resting) in Mild Cognitive Impairment patients, generating context-aware prompts for large language models to deliver real-time voice assistance [^2609.22024].

## Computational Neuroimaging and Foundation Models

The integration of foundation models and scalable simulation is transforming structural and functional neuroimaging. BrainFedFM introduces a dual-priority federated pretraining strategy for structural brain MRI, achieving state-of-the-art performance across diverse classification and segmentation tasks without pooling raw images [^2609.27611]. At the mechanistic level, CerebroSim enables scalable whole-brain spiking simulation at the 100-trillion-synapse scale on supercomputers, utilizing delay-aware spike broadcast and sparse synapse storage compression to make biologically constrained human-brain models practical for disorder studies [^2609.27482]. Image reconstruction techniques are also advancing rapidly; Directional Total Variation-Regularized Implicit Neural Representations (DTV-INR) integrate coordinate-driven networks with anisotropic regularizers to achieve continuous super-resolution in clinical brain MRI, circumventing staircasing artifacts [^2609.25429]. Similarly, uncertainty-aware 3D residual wavelet diffusion models address memory bottlenecks in ultra low-field MRI super-resolution, generating per-voxel uncertainty maps that highlight anatomical ambiguity while preserving disease-relevant atrophy [^2609.25319]. Motion correction in magnetic resonance fingerprinting has been enhanced by compact spiral navigators, enabling sub-second motion estimation and improving quantitative map reliability under rapid, unpredictable motion [^2609.25313]. Methodologically, additive nonparametric regression frameworks incorporating Gaussian process priors effectively predict task-based brain activation maps by modeling intricate functional dependencies between structural, resting-state, and task-fMRI data [^2609.26867]. In developmental contexts, deep learning is shifting infant functional neuroimaging from group-level analyses toward reliable, individualized trajectory forecasting [^2609.26688]. Comparative studies using EEG encoding models reveal that vision transformers sustain brain-predictive correspondence at deeper network layers better than CNNs, largely due to preserved patch-token representations [^2609.26512]. Additionally, functional causal discovery via conditional covariance ordering provides a distribution-free approach to estimating brain effective connectivity directed acyclic graphs [^2609.27256]. Foundational architectures are being adapted for brain-specific challenges, such as Brain-Inspired Hierarchical Modularity, which coordinates continual learning through expert specialization and ensemble integration inspired by the Drosophila learning system, consistently improving performance under online and uncertain data streams across vision and embodied tasks [^2609.25146]. Evaluating neuroimaging foundation models on underrepresented cohorts reveals that frozen backbones often collapse to majority-class predictions on non-western clinical brain MRI datasets, underscoring the need for parameter-efficient adaptation and broader external validation [^2609.23983]. At the network topology level, scalable generative frameworks superimposing metric decay with latent functional geometry successfully reconstruct hidden node coordinates in the Drosophila whole-brain connectome, uncovering an architectural hierarchy from sensory peripheries to associative neuropils [^2609.23933]. Functional connectivity analysis is being reformulated through edge-centric brain transformers that construct edge time-series representations to capture dynamic co-fluctuation patterns, organizing discriminative connections into line graphs for explicit connection-to-connection modeling in rs-fMRI [^2609.23782]. For cellular reconstruction, SomaNet employs a teacher-student paradigm with affinity learning to achieve accurate, weakly supervised 3D electron microscopy soma instance segmentation under partial annotation constraints [^2609.23019]. Training efficiency for latent world models is also being optimized; Mask-Aware JEPA execution separates mask-independent computation from routing, reducing memory traffic and achieving up to 1.7x speedup for brain dynamics and time series training [^2609.22674]. Medical imaging synthesis benefits from AWR-Net, which decouples anatomical correspondence from ultrasound appearance using wavelet diffusion and residual refinement to generate realistic 3D fetal brain volumes from MRI label maps [^2609.22635]. Unsupervised anomaly detection frameworks validated on brain MRI benchmarks achieve strong detection performance by inferring corruption parameters as probabilistic anomaly scores, producing estimates of pathology intensity and geometry [^2609.21800]. Submillimeter fMRI reliability is enhanced by Voxel-Matching NORDIC, which gathers similar non-local voxels based on time-series similarity to boost signal redundancy and significantly improve temporal SNR without inducing excessive spatial smoothness [^2609.21517]. Accelerated brain MRI acquisition is improved by Anatomy-aware Residual Attention Networks that refine SENSE reconstructions through generalized self-attention, preserving anatomical fidelity for quantitative measurements [^2609.22390]. Finally, forecasting intrathecal tracer enhancement from pre-contrast brain MRI demonstrates that direct image-to-image regression outperforms conditional flow matching in accuracy and computational cost, predicting enhancement from anatomy and timing alone [^2609.22377].

## Neurodegenerative Disorders and Brain Tumors

Understanding and treating brain pathologies continues to drive methodological innovation. Low-level blast exposure in veterans has been linked to statistically significant increases in depression and mental health symptoms, independent of PTSD or alcohol use, suggesting cumulative overpressure accelerates neurodegenerative or affective decline [^2609.25279]. Automated segmentation of brain metastases, which are often small and heterogeneous, benefits from multi-architecture comparisons; transformer-based models like Primus and residual encoder variants show complementary strengths in aggregate Dice scores versus lesion-wise F1 metrics [^2609.24769]. Unsupervised anomaly detection in medical scans is being reformulated as a Bayesian inverse problem with diffusion priors, jointly inferring pseudo-healthy images and spatial anomaly masks to improve localization on FDG PET and FLAIR MRI [^2609.24265]. In Alzheimer's disease research, the amyloid precursor protein (APP) is increasingly viewed as a universal pro-survival molecule rather than merely a pathological substrate, raising critical questions about the safety of broad-spectrum APP suppression therapies [^10.5281_zenodo.20049820]. Liquid biopsy approaches for glioblastoma multiforme (GBM) are advancing through cfDNA analysis; nucleosome repositioning landscapes and repeat-based cfDNA profiling offer non-invasive diagnostic signatures that mirror tumor-specific chromatin reprogramming [^10.5526_err-00042862][^10.5526_err-00042900]. Spatially resolved single-cell datasets for pan-cancer brain metastases have been released to facilitate microenvironment mapping [^10.5281_zenodo.21816284][^10.5281_zenodo.21782885]. Therapeutic delivery across the blood-brain barrier is being addressed via lipid nanoparticle formulations encapsulating siRNA targeting TERT or loaded with anticancer phytochemicals, demonstrating robust gene silencing and cytotoxic effects in GBM cell lines [^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059253][^10.17030_uclan.thesis.00059033]. Furthermore, RET signaling pathways have been identified as mediators of estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer brain metastasis [^10.5281_zenodo.19501083], while secretomes and extracellular vesicles from neural crest-derived stem cells show promise in inhibiting GBM proliferation and modulating inflammatory microenvironments [^10.48683_1926.00128572]. Finally, aerobic exercise is proposed to reshape nitric oxide signaling toward adaptive profiles that may enhance mitophagy and provide neuroprotection in Parkinson's disease [^10.4103_mgr.medgasres-d-26-00008]. Automated tumor segmentation continues to advance with VGG16-MCA UNet, which pairs an ImageNet-pretrained encoder with a decoder-side multi-channel attention module to achieve high pixel-level Dice scores for whole-tumor segmentation in 2D FLAIR MRI [^2609.23919]. Interactive 3D lesion segmentation is being enhanced by BrainIAC, a unified framework integrating multi-modal backends, bounding-box/click prompts, and online adaptation mechanisms that generalize across heterogeneous imaging modalities and unseen pathologies [^2609.23026]. Survival prediction for glioblastoma multiforme is improved by a multi-modal framework combining sheaf hypergraph neural networks for higher-order tissue patch relationships, concept bottleneck layers for ante-hoc interpretability, and extension sufficiency test regularization to ensure faithful explanations [^2609.25088]. Clinical gait analysis and neurodegenerative disease monitoring benefit from diagonal segment extraction techniques that transform dynamic time warping into an interpretable tool for localizing coordination breakdowns [^2609.24223]. In Parkinson's disease, implanted neurostimulators storing local field potentials face quantization artifacts that corrupt spectral biomarkers; dequantization via interval-censored subspace estimation reduces spurious peak detection while preserving true oscillatory activity [^2609.22658]. Post-cardiac arrest neuroprotection is explored through artemisinin treatment, which alleviates hippocampal neuronal apoptosis and cognitive dysfunction in rats by modulating the PI3K/Akt signaling pathway [^10.36721_pjps.2026.39.12.334.1]. The cellular architecture of breast cancer brain metastases is being mapped using multimodal tissue cytometry and spatial transcriptomics, revealing prognostically distinct immune niches that inform personalized treatment strategies [^10.5281_zenodo.17723783]. Drug delivery across the blood-brain barrier is being advanced through medical microbubbles combined with ultrasound, offering potential for targeted gene therapy and cancer treatment [^10.5281_zenodo.19676283]. Clinical outcomes for brain metastases from colorectal cancer treated with stereotactic radiosurgery show higher local failure rates compared to other primaries, highlighting the radioresistant biology of this histology [^10.1093_nop_npag050]. Environmental factors are also linked to neurodegeneration, as differential DNA methylation in blood mediates the association between ambient PM2.5 exposure and cerebrospinal fluid biomarkers of Alzheimer's disease [^10.1038_s41380-026-03662-9]. Stereotactic radiosurgery for thalamic brain metastases demonstrates sufficient local control and high survival rates over time, supporting its role as an effective treatment option [^10.1227_neuprac.0000000000000284]. Preoperative frailty measures for vestibular schwannoma surgeries indicate that tumor size, rather than frailty indices, is the dominant correlate of early postoperative outcomes like length of stay and facial nerve function [^10.1227_neuprac.0000000000000289].

## Cognitive Neuroscience and Developmental Dynamics

Cognitive and developmental frameworks are being enriched by computational and molecular insights. AI-driven neural surrogate frameworks combine fMRI decoding with constrained latent-space steering to propose and test predicted perceptual effects for cognitive-affective neuromodulation, offering a falsifiable upstream method for designing psychiatric intervention targets [^2609.27729]. The concept of task-based neurons challenges traditional architecture designs, arguing that customized neurons capture task-specific prior knowledge; the TNLearn package facilitates automated construction and training of these networks [^2609.27564]. Theoretical models also explore emotions as intrinsic colored noise in biological networks, demonstrating how decision dynamics and opinion formation shift under varying imitation effects and memory ranges [^2609.25970]. Cross-cultural and aging studies reveal that category fluency performance is shaped by both gray matter volume variations in semantic control regions and culturally distinct cognitive categorization strategies [^10.48617_etd.1671]. In motor control, brain-based digital twins fuse EEG with kinematic data to model skilled interceptive movements, providing real-time neural efficiency feedback through synthetic data augmentation and closed-loop intervention [^10.17870_bathspa.00017805]. At the molecular level, circular RNAs accumulate in the Drosophila brain at rates corresponding to biological age, serving as stable biomarkers of the aging process and insulin-related pathways [^10.48617_etd.1568]. Systemic metabolism also intersects with brain function, as arteriovenous metabolomics profiling reveals significant organ-specific uptake and release of circulating metabolites, including those relevant to cerebral physiology [^10.5281_zenodo.20652506].

## Neural Signaling and Circuit Mechanisms

Fundamental circuit mechanisms continue to be elucidated through targeted signaling studies. Cholecystokinin (CCK) signaling in the nucleus of the solitary tract (NTS) plays a critical role in satiety and energy balance; NTS-CCK neurons are directly connected to vagal afferents and can release local CCK to activate downstream neurons, with high-fat diets shown to blunt central CCK signaling in males [^10.7273_000008411]. Olfactory system organization is also being redefined, revealing that each olfactory receptor is expressed at a unique mean dorsoventral position in the epithelium, instantiating a stereotyped receptor map that aligns sensory maps in the nose and brain through a coherent gene expression program [^10.5281_zenodo.18941307]. Generative modeling of neural population activity is advancing through discrete models that represent array-wide spiking activity using a shared vocabulary of spatiotemporal motifs learned via residual vector-quantized autoencoders, achieving superior reconstruction and generation metrics without assay-specific parameters [^2609.23907].
