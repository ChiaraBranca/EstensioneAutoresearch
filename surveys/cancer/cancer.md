# Living Survey: cancer

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **cancer**.

## Diagnostic Imaging and AI-Driven Analysis
Recent advances in foundation models and computer vision have significantly enhanced cancer screening and diagnosis. Foundation model embeddings of screening mammograms have been shown to encode pre-diagnostic tissue changes without task-specific adaptation, with malignant cases drifting faster along a "cancer direction" in embedding space than controls [^2609.26605]. Similarly, the Mammo-LIFE framework integrates longitudinal mammographic features with clinical variables to predict post-radiotherapy outcomes, utilizing low-rank adaptation and longitudinal comparison modules [^2609.26443]. In pathology, the WILSON vision-language foundation model represents whole-slide images as multi-magnification composites, outperforming dedicated case-level models and generating diagnostic text with high recall [^2609.25123]. For ultrasound analysis, FreqDINO++ introduces a frequency-guided multi-task routing adapter to bridge the domain gap from natural images, achieving robust performance across 27 clinical tasks [^2609.20340]. Furthermore, counterfactual explanations guided by Formal Concept Analysis (FCA-CF) have achieved perfect validity and emergent sparsity in multi-modal breast cancer diagnosis, offering clinically actionable insights where traditional attribution methods fail [^2609.20067]. Spatial transcriptomics benchmarking reveals that active learning strategies for spot selection do not consistently outperform random sampling at small budgets, highlighting the complexity of high-dimensional continuous expression vectors [^2609.27208]. Microbiome-based cancer detection benchmarks (BreCol) demonstrate that classical models outperform deep learning approaches on holdout data, emphasizing the value of unsupervised clustering for compositional signals [^2609.27207]. Endoscopic imaging datasets like ERCPMP-Gx link polyposis phenotypes to histopathology and germline findings, supporting AI-driven classification of hereditary syndromes [^2609.20815]. Finally, hybrid quantum-classical models on the Breast Cancer Wisconsin dataset show that classical classifiers currently surpass quantum variants in accuracy, though quantum models exhibit competitive benign-class recall [^2609.23476].

## Tumor Evolution and Microenvironment Dynamics
Understanding the physical and evolutionary mechanisms driving tumor progression is critical. Tumor-induced lipid loss physically remodels white adipose tissue, accelerating breast cancer invasion into mammary fat, a process exacerbated by obesity-associated tissue pressure [^2609.23229]. At the clonal level, mutation order and selection shape intratumor heterogeneity; a strong driver acquired early expands intermediate lineages, whereas late acquisition strengthens early-founded clones, potentially producing non-monotone relationships between selective advantage and clonal concentration [^2609.25838]. Mechanistic modeling of tumor growth using 2D reaction-diffusion equations allows for the estimation of cell diffusivity and proliferation rates from single-point biopsies, providing potential biomarkers for radiotherapy risk-stratification [^2609.22363]. Additionally, non-parametric estimators for excess recurrent events extend relative survival analysis to recurrent hospitalizations, evaluated using rectal cancer data [^2609.26328].

## Genomic, Proteomic, and Molecular Profiling
Multi-omics approaches are uncovering novel biomarkers and regulatory networks. A pan-cancer co-expression network analysis identified hub genes with potential for predicting drug responses [^10.5281_zenodo.2574643]. Single-cell and spatial multi-omics revealed that ccRCC lung metastases harbor cancer-associated fibroblast lymphocyte exclusion sites forming confined tumor microenvironments [^10.5281_zenodo.18767278]. In glioblastoma, nucleosome repositioning in cell-free DNA (cfDNA) from liquid biopsies provides a non-invasive readout of disease state, with repeat-based profiling achieving high diagnostic accuracy [^10.5526_err-00042900]. The chromatin-associated DEK oncogene promotes metastatic melanoma by modulating chromatin accessibility and transcriptional reprogramming, regulated by CK2-mediated phosphorylation [^10.17638_03194520]. Deubiquitinating enzymes (DUBs) like USP42 regulate YAP/TAZ activity in mesothelioma and head and neck cancers, offering potential therapeutic targets [^10.17638_03193849]. Proteome-wide mapping identified stable transcriptomic predictors of protein abundance in cancer cell lines, revealing context-specific gene features [^2609.24004]. Inferred hub miRNAs (HubmiRs) drive cross-task improvements in drug mechanistic modeling, outperforming transcription factor activity in perturbational drug modeling [^2609.21280]. Bayesian posterior learning of mixed graphical models applied to breast cancer gene expression reveals meaningful dependencies between profiles and cancer subtypes [^2609.23857]. Hybrid quantum-classical attention mechanisms for histopathology-based molecular profiling show selective gains in data-limited cancer cohorts, redistributing predictive accuracy across biologically relevant targets [^2609.21115]. Single-cell spatial atlases of pan-cancer metastatic brain tumors provide comprehensive multi-omic resources for studying tumor-immune ecosystems [^10.5281_zenodo.21816284][^10.5281_zenodo.21782885]. Peptide-HLA binding prediction for neoantigen identification benefits from hybrid quantum-classical neural networks that improve sample efficiency in low-data regimes [^2609.19642]. Prostate cancer screening pilot analysis using THz spectroscopy of urine vapors identifies candidate spectral features complementary to PSA testing [^2609.23814].

## Therapeutic Strategies and Clinical Applications
Novel therapeutic frameworks and clinical trial methodologies are being optimized. A federated AI framework for pancreatic cancer treatment addresses GDPR boundaries and partially overlapping features across distributed sites, showing preliminary success in subtype identification [^2609.24718]. For esophageal cancer, an uncertainty-quantified ensemble model predicts severe radiotherapy-induced immunosuppression (G4RIL), supporting personalized treatment decisions like proton therapy selection [^2609.25139]. In breast cancer, polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFAs) mediate the antitumor effects of fasting by inducing lipid peroxidation and ferroptosis, supporting combined PUFA supplementation and cyclic fasting for TNBC [^10.21954_ou.ro.00108036]. Non-genotoxic topoisomerase II catalytic inhibitors have been developed and characterized for targeted cancer therapy [^10.14288_1.0451818]. Prostate cancer cells exhibit growth inhibition and lethality when treated with omega-3 fatty acids (EPA), which modulate mitochondrial succinylation networks [^10.17638_03195029]. Multi-omic prognostic modeling across 10,000 checkpoint blockade cases dissects tumor-immune ecosystems to identify prognostic cellular programs [^10.5281_zenodo.18706180]. Transportability methods combine routine care data with trial summary measures to estimate absolute treatment effects on the treated (ATT) for breast cancer endocrine therapy [^2609.24305]. Structured screen-and-select variable selection (S3VS) improves variable recovery in ultra-high-dimensional ovarian cancer data by exploiting predictor dependence [^2609.24945].

## Clinical Cohorts and Epidemiological Insights
Large-scale cohort studies and epidemiological analyses continue to inform cancer risk and management. Comprehensive RNA-seq analysis of a Korean colorectal cancer cohort identified differentially expressed genes enriched in cell cycle and IL-17 pathways, alongside Consensus Molecular Subtype correlations [^10.5281_zenodo.8307739]. The built environment's association with cancer risk highlights the role of spatial and societal factors in oncology [^10.14288_1.0451801]. Long-term tail modeling in survival analysis via extended generalized Pareto distributions integrates extreme value theory with survival analysis, revealing trade-offs between flexibility and stability in tail-index estimation under censoring [^2609.28321].
