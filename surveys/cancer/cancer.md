# Living Survey: cancer

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **cancer**.

## Molecular Mechanisms & Tumor Microenvironment
Recent studies have elucidated complex biophysical and metabolic interactions within the tumor microenvironment. Tumor-induced lipid loss has been shown to physically remodel adipose tissue, accelerating breast cancer invasion through increased tissue pressure [^2609.23229]. Similarly, dense HeLa cell monolayers exhibit liquid-like collective dynamics that sustain metastatic potential even under strong crowding conditions [^2609.18768]. Metabolic vulnerabilities are also being targeted; omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids induce mitochondrial succinylation networks that disrupt homeostasis and trigger ferroptosis in prostate cancer cells [^10.17638_03195029]. At the chromatin level, the DEK oncoprotein drives metastatic melanoma progression through context-dependent transcriptional regulation and phosphorylation-dependent chromatin remodeling [^10.17638_03194520]. Furthermore, deubiquitinating enzymes (DUBs) have been identified as critical regulators of YAP/TAZ oncogenic activity in mesothelioma and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, offering new therapeutic targets [^10.17638_03193849]. Metabolic modeling also extends to the gut microbiome, where a combined ODE model demonstrates that butyrate production from fiber fermentation can suppress colorectal tumor growth [^2609.19371].

Recent advances in nanotheranostics demonstrate that shape- and cation-engineered ferrite nanoparticles can significantly enhance heating and MRI contrast in ovarian cancer phantoms while maintaining hemocompatibility [^2609.14720]. Magnetic particle spectroscopy further enables sensitive detection of these nanoparticles within ovarian cancer cells, revealing partial restriction of Brownian rotation in the cellular environment [^2609.14162]. At the cellular signaling level, tissue microstructure profoundly influences calcium wave propagation, with new fluorescent biosensors providing dynamic readouts of protease secretion relevant to cancer progression [^10.48617_etd.1484]. In glioblastoma, secretomes and extracellular vesicles derived from neural crest stem cells inhibit tumor proliferation and modulate inflammatory signaling pathways, offering a novel immunomodulatory strategy [^10.48683_1926.00128572]. Spatial transcriptomics continues to unravel tumor heterogeneity; neuro-related tertiary lymphoid structures have been linked to chemotherapy sensitivity in pancreatic cancer [^10.5281_zenodo.17760849], while intra-tumor malignant cell diversity in colorectal cancer is shaped by microenvironmental cues [^10.5281_zenodo.17826866]. Immune-metabolic crosstalk is also being clarified, with Siglec-10 identified as a critical receptor protecting tumor-associated macrophages from lactate-induced impairment in colorectal metastasis [^10.5281_zenodo.21882301]. Metastatic mechanisms are further illuminated by clonal tracing experiments mapping breast cancer niches in the lung and liver [^10.5281_zenodo.10457442], and RET signaling is established as a key mediator of estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer brain metastasis [^10.5281_zenodo.19501083]. Finally, foundational DNA damage studies show that 5-methylcytosine significantly increases susceptibility to UV-induced cyclobutane pyrimidine dimers, advancing our understanding of mutational hotspots in skin carcinogenesis [^10.7273_000007851].

## Computational Oncology & AI Diagnostics
Artificial intelligence is rapidly transforming cancer diagnostics and computational modeling. Federated learning frameworks are being deployed to optimize pancreatic cancer treatment strategies while adhering to data privacy regulations [^2609.24718]. In histopathology, hybrid quantum-classical attention mechanisms improve molecular profiling in data-limited cancer cohorts by redistributing predictive accuracy toward biologically relevant targets [^2609.21115]. For medical imaging, FreqDINO++ introduces a frequency-guided multi-task routing foundation model that enhances universal ultrasound analysis for cancer screening [^2609.20340]. Explainability remains a clinical bottleneck; the FCA-Guided Counterfactual framework achieves perfect validity and emergent sparsity for multi-modal breast cancer diagnosis [^2609.20067]. Efficient architectures like the lightweight CNN-Compact Convolutional Transformer enable robust breast cancer mammography classification with minimal computational overhead [^2609.18212], while NeuroTS-Net advances multi-class semantic segmentation for pediatric brain tumors in multi-modal MRI [^2609.16873]. Mechanistic tumor growth parameters can now be estimated from single-point biopsies using 2D reaction-diffusion simulations [^2609.22363]. Additionally, comparative studies benchmark quantum versus classical machine learning models on structured medical datasets, highlighting current limitations and future trajectories for quantum classifiers in oncology [^2609.23476]. Public datasets like ERCPMP-Gx are also expanding AI readiness for colorectal polyposis characterization [^2609.20815].

The integration of vision-language models is streamlining complex imaging workflows, with a unified architecture now capable of simultaneous report generation, visual question answering, and lesion segmentation for PSMA PET/CT in prostate cancer [^2609.15603]. Weakly supervised spatial grounding techniques are improving ultrasound-histopathology alignment for prostate cancer grading by decoupling discriminative feature learning from attention pooling [^2609.15150]. For lung cancer, foundation models adapted for Lung-RADS screening demonstrate that fine-tuning bridges the gap between raw model capabilities and clinical consistency, though deterministic outputs trade off inter-reader variability [^2609.22281]. Robust partially observable Markov decision process frameworks are being developed to optimize low-dose CT screening schedules under transition-model uncertainty [^2609.12007], while Gaussian radial intensity priors guide novel architectures like GRIPNet to detect sub-six-millimeter pulmonary nodules with high precision [^2609.11312]. Large-scale annotation efforts, such as DICOM-harmonized segmentations for the National Lung Screening Trial, are enriching public datasets to accelerate algorithm development [^2609.10858]. Cross-modal translation is advancing through latent Brownian bridge diffusion models that synthesize high-fidelity PET volumes from CT scans, preserving small-lesion metabolic activation [^2609.12860]. In gynecological oncology, dual cross-attention frameworks are automating cervical intraepithelial neoplasia grading and Swede score prediction from colposcopic images [^2609.12827]. Breast imaging benefits from interpretable few-shot prototypical learning (ProtoCAM) for ultrasound lesion classification [^2609.13340] and physics-based triple Debye dielectric modeling to enhance terahertz pulse reflection signatures for early detection [^2609.12701]. Conformal prediction methods are also being adapted to handle transitional uncertainty in melanoma and oral lesion classification, converting ambiguous prediction sets into actionable clinical decisions [^2609.13303]. Machine unlearning frameworks like GRIN+ are addressing privacy regulations by precisely erasing sensitive patient data from models trained on imbalanced medical datasets like skin cancer and breast ultrasound [^2609.15571]. Furthermore, standardized DICOM encoding for computational pathology image-derived data is overcoming historical sharing bottlenecks [^2609.14530]. Unsupervised deep-learning approaches for low-dose CT reconstruction are also being developed to mitigate radiation risks while preserving diagnostic image quality [^10.17638_03194237].

## Genomics & Biomarker Discovery
High-dimensional genomic data continues to drive biomarker identification and spatial multi-omics. The S3VS framework improves variable recovery in ultra-high-dimensional settings by exploiting predictor correlations, demonstrating strong performance in ovarian cancer data [^2609.24945]. Proteome-wide mapping across cancer cell lines reveals stable transcriptomic predictors of protein abundance, enabling interpretable modeling even when proteomic data is scarce [^2609.24004]. Bayesian inference methods, such as the Mixed Graph WWA algorithm, facilitate scalable structure learning for heterogeneous gene expression data [^2609.23857]. Non-invasive diagnostics are advancing through THz spectroscopy of urine vapors for prostate cancer detection [^2609.23814] and semi-supervised learning pipelines for multi-stage hepatocellular carcinoma prediction [^2609.17100]. Pan-cancer analyses are yielding valuable insights: a co-expression network identifies hub genes predictive of drug responses [^10.5281_zenodo.2574643], while spatial multi-omics reveals lymphocyte exclusion sites in ccRCC lung metastases [^10.5281_zenodo.18767278]. Liquid biopsy techniques are being refined through cfDNA nucleosome positioning analysis, establishing repeat-based signatures for pan-cancer and tissue-specific diagnostics [^10.5526_err-00042900], and nucleosome repositioning landscapes are being mapped for glioblastoma multiforme [^10.5526_err-00042862]. Single-cell spatial atlases are also being constructed for pan-cancer metastatic brain tumors [^10.5281_zenodo.21816284], alongside comprehensive RNA-seq cohorts for colorectal cancer [^10.5281_zenodo.8307739] and urinary proteomics for breast cancer biomarker discovery [^10.5281_zenodo.20393677][^10.5281_zenodo.20405726].

Evolutionary search algorithms are being leveraged to accelerate hypothesis discovery in oncology; HypoEvolve coordinates specialized LLM agents to propose and refine drug repurposing hypotheses for cancer types, outperforming single-pass generation baselines [^2609.15938]. Multi-modal survival prediction is being enhanced by graph-guided mixture of experts frameworks that automatically route clinical, genomic, and histopathological data to specialized models, effectively handling missing modalities in lung adenocarcinoma [^2609.14072]. Spatial transcriptomics datasets are expanding rapidly, including a 1,000-plex CosMx dataset for colorectal cancer tissue microarrays that captures single-cell resolution metadata and fluorescence images [^10.5281_zenodo.21570353].

## Clinical Therapeutics & Immunotherapy
Translational research is focusing on optimizing treatment efficacy and understanding drug mechanisms. Transportability frameworks are being developed to estimate absolute treatment effects in target populations by combining routine care data with trial summary measures, applied here to breast cancer endocrine therapy [^2609.24305]. Drug mechanism-of-action modeling is enhanced by inferred hub-miRNAs, which provide biologically informed recoding of transcriptomic data for perturbational drug discovery [^2609.21280]. In immunotherapy, hybrid quantum-classical neural networks improve sample efficiency for peptide-HLA binding prediction, a critical step in neoantigen identification for personalized cancer vaccines [^2609.19642]. Multi-omic prognostic modeling is scaling to over 10,000 checkpoint blockade treatment cases, dissecting tumor-immune ecosystems to identify prognostic cellular programs [^10.5281_zenodo.18706180]. Nutritional interventions are also being explored; polyunsaturated fatty acids mediate the antitumor effects of fasting by inducing lipid peroxidation and ferroptosis in breast cancer [^10.21954_ou.ro.00108036].

Optimal control strategies for chemotherapy are being revolutionized by hybrid quantum particle swarm optimization, which approximates global control trajectories to overcome local minima and ensure smooth, biologically consistent drug delivery schedules [^2609.11197]. Dual cancer-selective lysosomally-targeted antibody-drug conjugates are expanding the druggable proteome by converting poor internalizers into potent therapeutics across multiple cancer targets [^10.5281_zenodo.21088424]. Regulatory science is also evolving to balance speed and certainty; empirical analyses of the FDA's Accelerated Approval pathway reveal that patients are willing to accept greater evidentiary uncertainty only when no alternatives exist or benefits are transformative, highlighting tensions in post-market oversight [^10.21953_lse.00004962].

## Epidemiology & Public Health
Access to advanced imaging is being democratized through mobile CT services deployed in rural and remote areas, with telehealth and regulatory-authorized AI identified as key integrators for underserved populations [^2609.14347]. Statistical modeling of screening programs addresses historical bias; Markov models demonstrate that differential detection in prostate cancer screening can artificially inflate observed risk ratios, necessitating inverse sensitivity analyses for accurate registry data [^2609.12376]. Trial design methodologies are also being optimized, with optimal cohort staircase designs providing analytical solutions for cluster randomized trials in psychosocial cancer care, balancing repeated measurement burdens with statistical precision [^2609.13740].
