# Living Survey: cancer

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **cancer**.

## AI-Driven Medical Imaging and Diagnostics

Recent advancements demonstrate the transformative impact of artificial intelligence on cancer imaging and diagnostic workflows. Deep learning architectures are achieving near-perfect accuracy in breast cancer mammography classification while maintaining clinical interpretability[^2609.18212]. Unified vision-language models are streamlining prostate cancer management by simultaneously generating reports, answering visual questions, and segmenting lesions in PSMA PET/CT scans[^2609.15603]. Counterfactual explanation frameworks leveraging Formal Concept Analysis are achieving perfect validity in multi-modal breast cancer diagnosis[^2609.20067]. Furthermore, autonomous robotic systems guided by occupancy networks are advancing, enabling margin-negative tumor resections in partial nephrectomy phantoms[^2609.16186]. Machine unlearning techniques are also being adapted to handle imbalanced medical datasets across skin, brain, and breast cancers without eroding vital clinical knowledge[^2609.15571]. Weakly supervised spatial grounding methods are improving ultrasound-histopathology alignment for prostate cancer grading[^2609.15150]. X-ray phase-contrast imaging capabilities are being optimized specifically for medical breast cancer applications[^2609.18048].

## Computational Pathology and Multi-Omics

The integration of multi-omics and computational pathology is revealing novel biomarkers and tumor microenvironments. Single-cell and spatial multi-omics have uncovered confined tumor microenvironments in ccRCC lung metastases[^10.5281_zenodo.18767278][^10.5281_zenodo.18767279]. Pan-cancer metastatic brain tumor atlases are being constructed to map cellular heterogeneity across multiple datasets[^10.5281_zenodo.21816284][^10.5281_zenodo.21816285][^10.5281_zenodo.21782885][^10.5281_zenodo.21782884]. Liquid biopsy techniques utilizing cell-free DNA nucleosome positioning signatures show robust potential for non-invasive early cancer detection and patient stratification[^10.5526_err-00042900]. Nucleosome repositioning landscapes in glioblastoma multiforme are being analyzed to identify potential biomarkers for liquid biopsies[^10.5526_err-00042862]. Semi-supervised learning frameworks are successfully identifying prognostic biomarkers for hepatocellular carcinoma across multiple disease stages[^2609.17100][^2609.15638]. Pan-cancer co-expression networks are highlighting hub genes with potential for predicting drug responses[^10.5281_zenodo.2574643]. Genomic studies are identifying cancer susceptibility alleles across diverse populations[^10.5281_zenodo.10453217][^10.5281_zenodo.10453218]. Comprehensive RNA-seq analyses are providing molecular insights into colorectal cancer cohorts[^10.5281_zenodo.8307739]. Multi-omic prognostic modeling is dissecting tumor-immune ecosystems across thousands of checkpoint blockade treatment cases[^10.5281_zenodo.18706180][^10.5281_zenodo.18706179]. Standardized DICOM-based harmonization is improving the sharing of computational pathology image-derived data[^2609.14530]. Pediatric brain tumor segmentation is being enhanced through novel encoder-decoder architectures[^2609.16873]. Endoscopic datasets are being developed to link polyposis phenotypes to histopathology and germline findings for colorectal cancer precursor lesions[^2609.20815].

## Therapeutic Discovery and Metabolic Interventions

AI and computational modeling are accelerating therapeutic discovery and understanding metabolic vulnerabilities. Genetic algorithms coordinating multi-agent LLMs are demonstrating superior performance in identifying drug repurposing candidates across 34 cancer types[^2609.15938]. Hybrid quantum-classical neural networks are improving sample efficiency for peptide-HLA binding prediction in personalized cancer immunotherapy[^2609.19642]. Metabolic interventions, such as omega-3 fatty acids, are shown to induce mitochondrial succinylation that triggers growth inhibition and ferroptosis in prostate cancer cells[^10.17638_03195029]. Fasting-mimicking diets combined with polyunsaturated fatty acids mediate antitumor effects in breast cancer by inducing lipid peroxidation[^10.21954_ou.ro.00108036]. Deubiquitinating enzymes (DUBs) are being identified as regulators of YAP/TAZ activity in mesothelioma and head and neck cancers[^10.17638_03193849]. The DEK oncogene is being characterized for its tumor-promoting roles and chromatin remodeling in metastatic melanoma[^10.17638_03194520]. Iron-catalyzed stereoselective glycosylation is enabling rapid synthesis of mucin-type glycopeptides for cancer vaccines[^10.48617_etd.1513]. Directed evolution of 2'-modified RNA is developing high-affinity aptamers for cancer-related protein targets[^10.48617_etd.1645]. Urinary proteomics are being utilized to identify potential biomarkers in breast cancer patients[^10.5281_zenodo.20393677]. Ovarian cancer theranostics are advancing through shape- and cation-engineered ferrite nanoparticles for magnetic hyperthermia and MRI[^2609.14720][^2609.14162]. Topoisomerase II catalytic inhibitors are being developed as non-genotoxic cancer therapies[^10.14288_1.0451818]. ODE models are simulating carbohydrate fermentation and its interaction with colorectal cancer cells[^2609.19371]. HeLa cell monolayer dynamics are providing insights into the metastatic potential of cancer cells under crowding conditions[^2609.18768]. Dietary cannabidiol is being studied for its role in modulating intestinal epithelial function and cancer risk[^10.7273_000008412].

## Data Infrastructure and Clinical Translation

Expanding access to cancer care and supporting research workflows are critical for clinical translation. Mobile CT services integrated with telehealth and regulatory-approved AI aim to bridge diagnostic gaps in remote and resource-limited regions[^2609.14347]. The built environment is being systematically evaluated for its association with cancer risk[^10.14288_1.0451801]. LLM-based clinical data abstraction tools are being co-designed to better support cancer research teams in distilling structured information from unstructured patient records[^2609.19318].
