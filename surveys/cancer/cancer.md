# Living Survey: cancer

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **cancer**.

## Recent Advances in Cancer Research

Recent literature has significantly expanded our understanding of cancer biology, diagnostics, and therapeutics through multi-omics, spatial transcriptomics, and metabolic interventions.

**Pan-cancer & Multi-omics Atlases:** Recent releases of spatial transcriptomics datasets have provided foundational resources for studying metastatic brain tumors [^10.5281_zenodo.21816284] [^10.5281_zenodo.21816285] [^10.5281_zenodo.21782885] [^10.5281_zenodo.21782884]. Concurrently, multi-omic prognostic modeling across over 10,000 checkpoint blockade cases has elucidated tumor-immune ecosystem dynamics and identified prognostic cellular programs [^10.5281_zenodo.18706180] [^10.5281_zenodo.18706179]. Pan-cancer co-expression networks have further identified hub genes with potential for predicting drug responses [^10.5281_zenodo.2574643].

**Liquid Biopsy & Diagnostics:** Non-invasive diagnostics are advancing rapidly. Liquid biopsies leveraging cell-free DNA (cfDNA) nucleosome positioning and repetitive element profiling have demonstrated high accuracy in discriminating cancer patients from healthy controls across multiple cancer types [^10.5526_err-00042862] [^10.5526_err-00042900]. Additionally, urinary proteomics has emerged as a viable approach for identifying breast cancer biomarkers [^10.5281_zenodo.20393677].

**Metabolic & Nutritional Interventions:** Metabolic plasticity remains a critical therapeutic vulnerability. Fasting and polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFAs) have been shown to induce ferroptosis and lipid peroxidation in breast cancer cells by modulating mitochondrial ROS production [^10.21954_ou.ro.00108036]. Similarly, omega-3 fatty acids (EPA) disrupt mitochondrial succinylation networks, inhibiting prostate cancer growth through aspartate biosynthesis depletion and ferroptosis induction [^10.17638_03195029].

**Oncogenic Mechanisms & Signaling:** Research continues to unravel critical signaling pathways driving malignancy. Deubiquitinating enzymes (DUBs) regulating YAP/TAZ activity were identified in mesothelioma and head and neck cancers, with USP42 highlighted as a potential therapeutic target [^10.17638_03193849]. The DEK oncoprotein's phosphorylation-dependent chromatin remodeling role in metastatic melanoma was also characterized, linking it to transcriptional repression and chromatin compaction [^10.17638_03194520]. Furthermore, clear cell renal cell carcinoma (ccRCC) lung metastases were found to harbor cancer-associated fibroblast lymphocyte exclusion sites that form confined tumor microenvironments [^10.5281_zenodo.18767278] [^10.5281_zenodo.18767279].

**Drug Discovery & Epidemiology:** Novel non-genotoxic topoisomerase II catalytic inhibitors for cancer therapy have been developed [^10.14288_1.0451818]. Transcriptomic analysis of colorectal cancer in Korean cohorts revealed distinct molecular subtypes and potential therapeutic targets [^10.5281_zenodo.8307739]. Finally, environmental factors, specifically the built environment, have been linked to cancer risk, emphasizing the role of external influences in oncogenesis [^10.14288_1.0451801].
