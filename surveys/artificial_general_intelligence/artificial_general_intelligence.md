# Living Survey: Artificial_General_Intelligence

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **Artificial_General_Intelligence**.

## Theoretical and Architectural Foundations

Recent research continues to explore the mathematical and algorithmic pathways toward autonomous general intelligence. The topology of mind has been redefined through morphogenetic continuity, establishing the Form F as a primary constituent of mind where consciousness emerges from the systemic integration of stochastic perturbations into stable gradients[^10.5281_zenodo.19034234]. This framework is further supported by parallel investigations into the structural absorption of energy for preserving topological integrity against dissolution[^10.5281_zenodo.19034233]. In parallel, deep reinforcement learning is being positioned as a general-purpose, platform-independent framework capable of generating the high-level control architectures necessary for AGI[^10.48550_arxiv.2105.13806]. Furthermore, the transition from narrow AI to general intelligence requires abandoning mere imitation of human problem-solving in favor of environmental interaction and knowledge accumulation[^10.20948_future-2023-16].

## Safety, Control, and Verification

Ensuring that AGI systems remain aligned with human values is a critical frontier. Provably safe systems are argued to be the only viable path to controllable AGI, relying on advanced formal verification and mechanistic interpretability to guarantee that systems satisfy human-specified requirements[^10.48550_arxiv.2309.01933]. From a policy perspective, the integration of AGI into sensitive domains like healthcare demands rigorous conceptual discipline. The term AGI is often contested and may mask unpredictable capabilities, necessitating a precautionary approach that aligns with existing regulatory frameworks like the EU AI Act[^10.1016_j.jrt.2026.100183].

## Consciousness and Synthetic Minds

The question of whether artificial systems can achieve genuine consciousness remains central to AGI discourse. Investigations into robot consciousness suggest that sufficiently advanced general robotic intelligence may become ambiguously indistinguishable from human consciousness, raising profound questions about theory of mind and existential roles[^10.22339_jbh.v7i4.7403]. Concurrently, speculation intensifies regarding the feasibility of machine consciousness, with researchers debating the thresholds at which AI systems might approximate subjective experience[^10.1109_mpuls.2024.3354609]. Beyond biological consciousness, the emergence of synthetic philosophy created by computer systems challenges traditional epistemological boundaries, prompting inquiries into the ethical responsibility for AI-generated philosophical works[^10.14746_cis.2024.58.2].

## Ethical, Societal, and Philosophical Implications

The pursuit of AGI carries significant ethical weight and societal risk. Critical analyses highlight that the normative framework driving AGI development is sometimes rooted in problematic historical ideologies, warning that undefined systems like AGI cannot be safely tested and may centralize power while harming marginalized groups under the guise of safety[^10.5210_fm.v29i4.13636]. The broader philosophical landscape connects AI advancement to transhumanism and posthumanism, examining how cognitive enhancements and super-intelligence could reshape individual differences and societal structures[^10.1016_j.intell.2021.101563]. Broader societal concepts emphasize the need for risk management and complementary human-machine cooperation to prevent uncontrolled substitution of labor, which could lead to unprecedented inequality or, conversely, free humanity from routine tasks[^10.3390_asi4020037]. Existential risk simulations further identify artificial intelligence superiority as a critical factor that could impede human civilization's advancement, requiring careful study alongside traditional threats like nuclear war and climate change[^10.20944_preprints202209.0061.v1]. Similar probabilistic models reinforce that AI risks, while abstract, demand rigorous attention to prevent catastrophic outcomes[^10.48550_arxiv.2209.02396].

## Human-AI Interaction and Strategic Development

As AGI research progresses, the focus shifts toward practical human-machine interaction and global strategic competition. Media linguistics research indicates that written interaction with large language models is rapidly evolving, setting the stage for future challenges posed by strong AI and artificial general intelligence[^10.21248_jfml.2026.117]. This evolution supports a paradigm of human-AI synergy, where computational power and data processing augment human intuition, creativity, and ethical reasoning to revolutionize intelligence across domains[^10.52783_jisem.v10i37s.6762]. On a geopolitical scale, national strategies are actively monitoring and funding paths to general AI, with specific focus on machine learning, brain-inspired AI, and brain-computer interfaces to secure leadership in the field[^10.51593_20210064].
