# Living Survey: Artificial_General_Intelligence

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **Artificial_General_Intelligence**.

## Recent Developments in AGI Research

**Policy, Ethics, and Societal Impact**
Recent discourse emphasizes the need for conceptual discipline and ethical frameworks surrounding AGI development. The European Commission's call for AGI in healthcare has sparked debate on risk management and the precautionary principle, highlighting that the term "AGI" is often contested and potentially problematic when applied to advanced AI without clear boundaries[^10.1016_j.jrt.2026.100183]. Furthermore, critical analyses point out that the pursuit of AGI is sometimes rooted in normative frameworks linked to historical eugenicist ideologies, warning that undefined "safe AGI" systems could centralize power and harm marginalized groups under the guise of benefiting humanity[^10.5210_fm.v29i4.13636].

**Architectural and Theoretical Foundations**
Theoretical advancements continue to explore the mathematical and topological underpinnings of autonomous intelligence. New frameworks propose replacing subjective units with formal morphogenetic structures to describe the transition from computation to genuine autonomy, arguing for the mathematical inevitability of AGI through topological integrity and structural absorption of energy[^10.5281_zenodo.19034234][^10.5281_zenodo.19034233]. Concurrently, research into the future of intelligence examines the philosophical movements of trans- and posthumanism, outlining how cognitive enhancements and brain-computer interfaces might converge with artificial super-intelligence to reshape human psychological capacities[^10.1016_j.intell.2021.101563].

**Safety, Control, and Strategic Development**
Ensuring the controllability of powerful AGI systems remains a paramount challenge. Proposals for "provably safe systems" advocate for advanced formal verification and mechanistic interpretability as the only viable path to guaranteeing that AGI satisfies human-specified requirements[^10.48550_arxiv.2309.01933]. Strategic monitoring efforts, such as those tracking China's national AI strategy, identify key institutions pursuing general AI capable of autonomous action in novel circumstances, emphasizing machine learning and brain-inspired architectures[^10.51593_20210064]. Additionally, discussions on the neural network revolution highlight the necessity of transitioning from imitation-based problem solving to environmental interaction to bridge the gap between narrow AI and general intelligence[^10.20948_future-2023-16].

**Robotics, Consciousness, and Cognitive Integration**
The intersection of robotics, consciousness, and AGI is increasingly scrutinized. Investigations into robot consciousness question whether sufficiently advanced general robotic intelligence could be indistinguishable from human consciousness, exploring implications for ethics, free will, and cosmological ontologies[^10.22339_jbh.v7i4.7403]. Parallel inquiries into AI consciousness debate the potential for artificial systems to achieve genuine subjective experience, framing it as a central speculative frontier in the field[^10.1109_mpuls.2024.3354609]. Finally, deep reinforcement learning frameworks are being adapted as general-purpose architectures for intelligent robot control, aiming to create platform-independent high-level control systems that incrementally improve intelligence through environmental feedback[^10.48550_arxiv.2105.13806].
