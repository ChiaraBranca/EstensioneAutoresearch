# Living Survey: Artificial_General_Intelligence

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **Artificial_General_Intelligence**.

## AGI Policy and Regulation

Recent discourse emphasizes the need for conceptual discipline and regulatory frameworks surrounding AGI development. The European Commission's call for AGI in healthcare has sparked debate on the risks and definitions of the term, highlighting the tension between advanced AI applications and the precautionary principle in EU policy[^10.1016_j.jrt.2026.100183]. Concurrently, strategic analyses track national initiatives, such as China's comprehensive roadmap aiming for leadership in general AI by 2030, underscoring the geopolitical race to achieve autonomous capabilities[^10.51593_20210064].

## AGI Consciousness and Robotics

The intersection of consciousness and general intelligence remains a pivotal area of inquiry. Theoretical frameworks propose that consciousness may emerge as a systemic capacity to integrate stochastic perturbations into stable gradients, positioning autonomous general intelligence as a mathematical inevitability within certain morphogenetic architectures[^10.5281_zenodo.19034234]. In parallel, investigations into robot consciousness explore whether sufficiently advanced general robotic intelligence could be indistinguishable from human consciousness, raising profound questions about substitution in responsible roles and the nature of artificial minds[^10.22339_jbh.v7i4.7403]. The broader question of whether AI systems can ever approach genuine consciousness continues to drive intense speculation and research[^10.1109_mpuls.2024.3354609].

## AGI Safety and Ethical Frameworks

Ensuring the safety of AGI is frequently framed as a paramount objective, though critics argue that undefined systems like AGI resist standard safety testing. Some analyses suggest that the drive toward AGI is rooted in normative frameworks that may perpetuate discriminatory attitudes, using the language of safety to centralize power while evading accountability for harms to marginalized groups[^10.5210_fm.v29i4.13636]. Conversely, other research advocates for a rigorous path to controllable AGI by building systems that provably satisfy human-specified requirements through advanced formal verification and mechanistic interpretability, arguing this is the only guaranteed route to safe deployment[^10.48550_arxiv.2309.01933].

## Philosophical and Strategic Perspectives on AGI

The pursuit of AGI intersects deeply with transhumanist and posthumanist philosophies, which envision technologies that strongly enhance human cognitive capacities. Debates continue regarding the promises and perils of super-intelligence, the commonalities between human and artificial intelligence, and how psychology can contribute to developing such systems[^10.1016_j.intell.2021.101563].
