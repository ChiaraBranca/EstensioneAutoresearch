## Recent Developments in AGI Policy, Safety, and Architecture

Recent policy discussions highlight the need for conceptual discipline regarding AGI, particularly in high-stakes domains like healthcare. The European Commission's recent calls for proposals on AGI for healthcare have sparked debate over the term's ambiguity and potential risks, emphasizing the need for the EU AI Act's precautionary approach to manage unquantifiable dangers associated with qualitatively novel AI technologies [^10.1016_j.jrt.2026.100183].

Theoretical frameworks continue to evolve, with recent work proposing a morphogenetic architecture for autonomous general intelligence. By defining the mental state as a rigorous morphogenetic structure emerging from compatible configurations and sensitive constraints, this approach demonstrates the mathematical inevitability of AGI, framing autonomy and survival instincts as the structural preservation of topological integrity against dissolution [^10.5281_zenodo.19034234].

Critical perspectives on AGI development emphasize the ethical and sociopolitical risks of pursuing undefined, all-knowing systems. Research has linked the normative framework driving AGI goals to historical eugenic traditions, warning that such pursuits may centralize power and harm marginalized groups under the guise of safety and human benefit, advocating instead for well-defined tasks with rigorous safety protocols [^10.5210_fm.v29i4.13636].

Ensuring the controllability of AGI remains a paramount challenge, with recent proposals advocating for provably safe systems as the only viable path. This approach leverages advanced AI for formal verification and mechanistic interpretability to guarantee that powerful AGIs satisfy human-specified requirements, outlining specific challenge problems to advance this critical safety research [^10.48550_arxiv.2309.01933].

Geopolitical strategies increasingly focus on achieving general AI capabilities, with national initiatives like China's 2030 roadmap actively funding research across machine learning, brain-inspired AI, and brain-computer interfaces to secure a leading position in autonomous general intelligence development [^10.51593_20210064].
