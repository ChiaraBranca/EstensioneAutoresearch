# Living Survey: apple

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **apple**.

## Apple Cultivation and Post-Harvest Science

Recent research has significantly advanced the understanding of apple physiology and agricultural robotics. Studies on 'Granny Smith' apples demonstrate that the chlorophyll-to-carotenoid ratio serves as a strong non-destructive predictor for sunscald susceptibility, with metabolomic profiling revealing coordinated defense mechanisms against photooxidative stress [^10.7273_000008353]. In parallel, agricultural automation is being refined through multi-functional robotic platforms capable of precise pruning and harvesting in complex orchard environments, utilizing point cloud data to map branch structures and optimize cycle times [^https:__openalex.org_W7204741959]. Furthermore, sustainability initiatives are exploring the valorization of apple co-products; thermomechanical treatments have been shown to effectively functionalize cell wall polysaccharides from apple waste, while fermentation processes using specific bacterial strains successfully convert waste apples into high-value platform chemicals like lactic and succinic acids [^https:__openalex.org_W7206080001][^10.1186_s13068-026-02807-w].

## Apple Inc. Ecosystem, Finance, and AI Hardware

The technological and financial dimensions of Apple Inc. continue to attract rigorous academic scrutiny. In financial forecasting, deep learning architectures, particularly Transformers, have demonstrated superior predictive capabilities over traditional statistical models and other machine learning approaches when analyzing Apple's stock price movements [^10.54254_2754-1169_2026.ld36549]. Complementary research highlights the importance of data preparation pipelines, showing that Random Forest regressors trained on properly sourced Yahoo!Finance data can robustly model Apple's stock trends [^10.5281_zenodo.18147252]. On the software and security front, recent investigations have exposed vulnerabilities in Apple Safari's local learning privacy defenses [^10.5281_zenodo.22163943]. Finally, hardware optimization for Apple Silicon remains a critical area of study, with new frameworks like VeloxQuant-MLX enabling fast KV-cache quantization for large language models, achieving up to 16x compression with near-lossless quality directly on Metal kernels [^10.5281_zenodo.20647294].
