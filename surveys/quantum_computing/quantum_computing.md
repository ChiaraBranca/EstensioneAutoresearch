# Living Survey: Quantum_Computing

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **Quantum_Computing**.

## Foundational Concepts and Architectures

Recent research continues to explore novel computational paradigms beyond traditional transistor and qubit systems. Atom computing has been proposed as an alternative where atoms replace transistors, utilizing controlled flows of single electrons or photons for deterministic switching, potentially overcoming material scaling constraints[^10.34257_gjsfra257362]. Furthermore, comparative analyses of future computing architectures highlight the distinct potential of quantum systems alongside digital and biological computers, emphasizing the need for standardized engineering descriptions and industry models[^10.1109_mc.2021.3083430].

## Quantum Algorithms and Optimization

The application of quantum algorithms to optimization problems remains a vibrant area of study. Hybrid and granular approaches have been introduced to enhance quantum genetic optimization, allowing digital computers to evaluate fitness functions while quantum processors evolve genetic populations, effectively reducing search space complexity even on limited-qubit devices[^10.1007_s41066-022-00348-9]. Additionally, empirical investigations into developer ecosystems reveal that hybrid quantum-classical computing and quantum circuit implementation are the most prevalent topics in practitioner discussions, with Qiskit and Q-sharp dominating the toolchain landscape alongside frequent references to Grover's and Shor's algorithms[^https:__openalex.org_W7155247427].

## Quantum Simulation and Materials Science

Quantum computing is increasingly applied to computational chemistry and materials science. Practical guides demonstrate how quantum annealers can calculate the relative energy of defective structures, such as graphene, by translating chemical models into qubit representations[^10.1063_5.0151346]. In the realm of fluid dynamics, quantum algorithms based on the Lattice Boltzmann Method have been developed to simulate 2D flows around cylinders, demonstrating the applicability of quantum computing to industrial CFD problems despite early-stage implementation challenges[^10.21741_9781644902813-38][^10.11159_icffts23.133]. Software tools like PULSEE also bridge quantum information science and magnetic resonance, simulating spin time evolution and quantum gates to investigate emergent quantum order in strongly correlated materials[^10.1016_j.cpc.2022.108598].

## Quantum Cryptography and Security

The intersection of quantum mechanics and cryptography continues to evolve. Educational and simulation works detail the implementation of the BB84 quantum key distribution (QKD) protocol, modeling quantum state preparation, eavesdropping attacks, and classical post-processing to demonstrate provably secure communication[^10.6084_m9.figshare.32051724]. Academic theses further explore quantum cryptography protocols (BB84, B92, B91) and Shor's algorithm, analyzing vulnerabilities such as photon splitting and faked-state attacks in quantum cryptographic systems[^https:__openalex.org_W7112574406]. Concurrently, the security landscape of post-quantum cryptography is being reassessed, with improved dual lattice attacks demonstrating that the security levels of leading LWE-based schemes like Kyber, Saber, and Dilithium may fall below NIST thresholds[^10.5281_zenodo.6493704]. Advanced encryption systems are also being designed to integrate post-quantum cryptographic strategies alongside classical algorithms to mitigate threats from Grover's and Shor's algorithms[^10.56975_tijer.v13i3.161368].

## Quantum Internet and Networking

The conceptualization of a global quantum internet is advancing, promising ultra-secure connectivity and the transmission of information encoded in quantum states across distributed quantum computers[^10.31695_ijerat.2025.9.1]. Tracking the emergence of these technologies, analyses of open software repositories and financial news provide pivotal insights into the evolving trends and industrial adoption of quantum computing[^10.1007_978-3-031-90727-2_20].

## Historical Perspectives and Applications

Foundational reviews continue to elucidate the core principles of quantum computing, explaining superposition, entanglement, and the transition from classical digital logic to qubit-based computation[^10.5120_ijca2021921834].
