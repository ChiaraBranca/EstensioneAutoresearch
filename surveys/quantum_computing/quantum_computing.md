# Living Survey: Quantum_Computing

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **Quantum_Computing**.

## Quantum Algorithms & Software Ecosystem
Recent studies have expanded the software landscape and algorithmic understanding of quantum systems. Developer engagement analysis reveals that hybrid quantum-classical computing and circuit implementation dominate community discussions, with Qiskit and Q-sharp being the most referenced toolchains alongside Grover's and Shor's algorithms `[^https:__openalex.org_W7155247427]`. In the domain of materials and spin dynamics, PULSEE introduces an open-source Python framework bridging quantum information science and magnetic resonance spectroscopy, enabling precise simulation of spin time evolution and quantum gates `[^10.1016_j.cpc.2022.108598]`. Furthermore, comprehensive reviews continue to map the prospective applications and stakeholder impacts of quantum computing, emphasizing superposition and entanglement as foundational paradigms `[^10.5120_ijca2021921834]`.

## Quantum Cryptography & Communication Networks
Secure communication remains a primary driver for quantum research. A detailed Python simulation of the BB84 QKD protocol has been published, modeling state preparation, Born rule measurements, intercept-resend eavesdropping, and classical post-processing stages like sifting and privacy amplification `[^10.6084_m9.figshare.32051724]`. Parallel theoretical work explores novel approaches to quantum cryptography, detailing protocols like BB84, B92, and B91, alongside Shor's algorithm implementation and security against photon-splitting and faked-state attacks `[^https:__openalex.org_W7112574406]`. The broader infrastructure vision is captured in primers on the quantum internet, which outline its theoretical foundations, ultra-secure connectivity potential, and the transition from classical to quantum state-based information transmission `[^10.31695_ijerat.2025.9.1]`.

## Quantum Computing Applications & Simulations
Quantum computing is increasingly applied to complex classical simulation problems. Reviews on computational fluid dynamics (CFD) discuss quantum algorithms for Navier-Stokes equations, Lattice Boltzmann methods, and analog quantum simulations tailored for aeronautical heat exchangers `[^10.11159_icffts23.133]` `[^10.21741_9781644902813-38]`. In materials science, practical guides demonstrate applying quantum annealing to calculate the relative energy of defective graphene structures, translating chemical models directly to qubit representations `[^10.1063_5.0151346]`. Additionally, hybrid granular computing approaches have been proposed for quantum genetic optimization, utilizing digital computers for fitness evaluation and quantum processors for population evolution to tackle continuous optimization problems `[^10.1007_s41066-022-00348-9]`.

## Quantum Hardware, Architectures & Future Trends
Architectural innovations continue to challenge classical scaling limits. A unified framework redefining matter and energy introduces "atom computing," where controlled electron and photonic flows replace transistors to enable deterministic switching beyond qubit-based constraints `[^10.34257_gjsfra257362]`. Comparative studies contextualize quantum computing's performance potential against traditional digital and biological architectures, providing standardized models for industry adoption `[^10.1109_mc.2021.3083430]`. Market and development trends are also being tracked through open-source repositories and financial news, highlighting the intersection of software development and economic sentiment in quantum technology adoption `[^10.1007_978-3-031-90727-2_20]`.

## Post-Quantum Cryptography & Security
The threat model posed by quantum computing has intensified research into post-quantum cryptography. Evaluations of Learning With Errors (LWE) based schemes (Kyber, Saber, Dilithium) present improved dual lattice attacks that successfully reduce their estimated security levels below NIST thresholds, underscoring the need for rigorous cryptanalysis in the quantum era `[^10.5281_zenodo.6493704]`.
