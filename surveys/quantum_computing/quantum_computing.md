# Living Survey: Quantum_Computing

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **Quantum_Computing**.

## Quantum Foundations and Alternative Architectures
Recent research continues to explore the fundamental boundaries of quantum mechanics and computational paradigms. A notable proposal introduces atom computing, suggesting that controlled flows of single electrons or photons could replace traditional transistors and qubits, offering a deterministic switching mechanism that bypasses current scaling constraints [^10.34257_gjsfra257362]. Broader surveys continue to map the expansive realms of quantum computing applications, emphasizing its counterintuitive yet powerful reliance on superposition and entanglement to disrupt classical digital logic [^10.5120_ijca2021921834]. Furthermore, comparative analyses of future computing paradigms highlight quantum systems alongside digital and biological architectures, stressing the need for standardized engineering descriptions to bridge theoretical potential with practical implementation [^10.1109_mc.2021.3083430].

## Quantum Algorithms and Simulation
The simulation of complex physical and chemical systems remains a primary driver for quantum algorithm development. Practical guides have emerged for applying quantum annealing to materials science, demonstrating how defective graphene structures can be modeled on quantum annealers to calculate relative energies intractable for classical computers [^10.1063_5.0151346]. In the realm of fluid dynamics, quantum computing is being adapted for Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) simulations, with approaches ranging from solving Navier-Stokes equations to implementing Lattice Boltzmann methods for 2D flow modeling around industrial components [^10.11159_icffts23.133][^10.21741_9781644902813-38]. Additionally, specialized software like PULSEE has been developed to simulate magnetic resonance observables, leveraging quantum information science protocols to investigate emergent quantum order in strongly correlated materials [^10.1016_j.cpc.2022.108598]. Optimization problems also benefit from quantum approaches, where hybrid granular computing schemes enhance quantum genetic algorithms to navigate continuous search spaces more effectively on current noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) devices [^10.1007_s41066-022-00348-9].

## Quantum Communication and Cryptography
Secure communication networks are undergoing a paradigm shift with the advent of quantum internet technologies. Primer studies outline the theoretical infrastructure required for a global quantum internet, promising ultra-secure connectivity and transformative capabilities for industries reliant on data transmission [^10.31695_ijerat.2025.9.1]. At the protocol level, academic works provide detailed implementations and security analyses of quantum key distribution (QKD) schemes, including BB84, B92, and B91, while evaluating vulnerabilities like intercept-resend and faked-state attacks [^10.6084_m9.figshare.32051724][https:__openalex.org_W7112574406]. The cryptographic implications of quantum computing are equally critical, with advanced hybrid encryption systems integrating post-quantum algorithms to counter threats from Grover’s and Shor’s algorithms [^10.56975_tijer.v13i3.161368]. Concurrently, cryptanalysis reports rigorously evaluate the security of post-quantum key exchange schemes like Kyber and Dilithium, demonstrating how improved dual lattice attacks reduce their effective security margins [^10.5281_zenodo.6493704].

## Quantum Ecosystem and Toolchains
As quantum technologies transition from theoretical research to industrial adoption, understanding the developer ecosystem becomes paramount. Empirical investigations mining developer discussions reveal that hybrid quantum-classical computing and circuit implementation dominate practitioner conversations, with frameworks like Qiskit and Q# leading the landscape [^https:__openalex.org_W7155247427]. Tracking emerging trends through open-source repositories and financial news further illustrates the growing momentum of quantum technologies in both academic and commercial sectors [^10.1007_978-3-031-90727-2_20].
