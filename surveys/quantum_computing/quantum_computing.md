# Living Survey: Quantum_Computing

## Introduction

This document collects the scientific literature regarding **Quantum_Computing**.

## Quantum Algorithms & Toolchains
Recent studies have focused on the practical adoption and developer ecosystems surrounding quantum technologies. An empirical investigation into Stack Overflow repositories highlights that hybrid quantum-classical computing and circuit implementation are the most prevalent topics, with Qiskit and Q-sharp dominating discussions [[^https:__openalex.org_W7155247427]]. Furthermore, tracking trends across open software platforms and financial news provides pivotal insights into the emerging quantum landscape [[^10.1007_978-3-031-90727-2_20]].

## Quantum Simulation & Optimization
Significant progress has been made in applying quantum paradigms to complex simulations and optimization problems. The PULSEE software package facilitates the simulation of magnetic resonance observables, bridging quantum information science with condensed matter research [[^10.1016_j.cpc.2022.108598]]. In optimization, granular computing schemes have been shown to statistically enhance quantum evolutionary algorithms for continuous functions [[^10.1007_s41066-022-00348-9]]. Additionally, quantum annealing has been practically applied to the configurational analysis of defective graphene structures, offering a guide for computational chemists [[^10.1063_5.0151346]].

## Quantum Cryptography & Internet
The development of secure quantum communication networks remains a critical frontier. Research outlines the theoretical foundations and practical steps toward a global Quantum Internet, promising ultra-secure connectivity for quantum devices [[^10.31695_ijerat.2025.9.1]]. Comprehensive analyses of quantum cryptography protocols, including BB84, B92, and B91, alongside Shor's algorithm implementations on quantum hardware, continue to refine security standards [[^https:__openalex.org_W7112574406]]. Practical simulations of the BB84 protocol, modeling eavesdropping and channel noise, further demonstrate the viability of provably secure communication [[^10.6084_m9.figshare.32051724]].

## Applications in Engineering & Materials
Quantum computing is increasingly explored for specialized engineering challenges. In computational fluid dynamics (CFD), quantum algorithms based on the Lattice Boltzmann Method and Navier-Stokes equations are being developed to simulate complex thermal fluid systems, such as aeronautical heat exchangers [[^10.11159_icffts23.133]][^10.21741_9781644902813-38]]. Beyond traditional computing architectures, novel paradigms like atom computing propose replacing transistors with controlled electron flows to overcome material scaling limits [[^10.34257_gjsfra257362]]. Comparative studies also continue to evaluate the architectural distinctions between digital, quantum, and biological computing systems [[^10.1109_mc.2021.3083430]].

## Post-Quantum Cryptography & Security
As quantum capabilities advance, the security of classical cryptographic standards is under scrutiny. The CypherGliph system introduces hybrid iterative cipher chaining combined with post-quantum cryptographic strategies to mitigate threats from Grover’s and Shor’s algorithms [[^10.56975_tijer.v13i3.161368]]. Concurrently, improved dual lattice attacks on Learning With Errors (LWE) problems have demonstrated reduced security margins for leading post-quantum finalists like Kyber and Dilithium, emphasizing the need for rigorous cryptanalysis [[^10.5281_zenodo.6493704]]. A comprehensive review of quantum computing applications also underscores the transformative potential and future disruptions across various scientific and industrial domains [[^10.5120_ijca2021921834]].
